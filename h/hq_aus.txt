DI: Il Central Otago è nell'entroterra, ai piedi delle Alpi Meridionali, con vigneti sparsi in diverse valli con esposizioni, suoli e altitudini diverse. DE: Central Otago is inland in the foothills of the Southern Alps, with vineyards spread across valleys of different aspects, soils and altitudes. Unlike the rest of (maritime) New Zealand, its climate is continental and frost is a real risk in spring and autumn. Summers can be warm but, importantly, the growing-season diurnal range is large. Sunlight intensity is very high, accounting for the high alcohol often found. Pinot Noir dominates: full-bodied, juicy, vibrant wines with concentrated ripe red fruit; other grapes: Pinot Gris, Riesling and Chardonnay.
