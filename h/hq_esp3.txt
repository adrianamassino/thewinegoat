In Rías Baixas, with a moderate, damp climate, most Albariño is unoaked, showing natural high acidity and ripe stone fruit; some are richer, occasionally with a touch of oak or lees stirring; red is permitted but a minority. Bierzo, mainly red, lies in the mountains between Galicia and the Meseta, with a moderate, maritime-cooled climate: Mencía gives elegant reds with high acidity and red fruit; many of the best come from old vines on steep stony slopes; some are unoaked, many use new oak for toasty notes.

DE: The Levante is the Mediterranean coastal region south of Catalunya; trade centres on the port of Valencia, the distribution hub for Levante and La Mancha wines. Valencia DO is a large, spread-out source of value wines with many local and international varieties: Monastrell is the most planted red, Merseguera dominates white plantings, and much Muscat of Alexandria makes Moscatel de Valencia, a sweet fortified wine. Further inland, hot, arid Jumilla and Yecla mostly make youthful, fruity Monastrell.

DI: Quasi metà del vino spagnolo viene da Castilla-La Mancha, vasta zona centrale della Meseta a sud di Madrid. La Mancha è la DO più grande di Spagna; l'Airén, la più piantata, dà bianchi neutri e freschi, ma è in calo perché le autorità hanno incoraggiato Tempranillo (Cencibel) e varietà internazionali come Cabernet, Merlot, Syrah, Chardonnay e Sauvignon. Grandi investimenti in tecnologia e competenze la rendono oggi un'ottima fonte di vini economici ben fatti per l'export; qui è nata anche la tendenza dei Vinos de Pago. Valdepeñas, subito a sud, ha lo stesso clima ma una reputazione di qualità superiore, con Cencibel in purezza o in taglio, da fruttati a più concentrati in legno.
DF: Près de la moitié du vin espagnol vient de Castille-La Manche, vaste zone de la Meseta au sud de Madrid. La Mancha est la plus grande DO d'Espagne ; l'airén, le plus planté, donne des blancs neutres et frais, mais il recule car les autorités encouragent le tempranillo (cencibel) et des internationaux comme cabernet, merlot, syrah, chardonnay et sauvignon. D'importants investissements en technologie et savoir-faire en font aujourd'hui une excellente source de vins bon marché bien faits pour l'export ; c'est aussi là qu'est née la tendance des Vinos de Pago. Valdepeñas, juste au sud, a le même climat mais une meilleure réputation, avec du cencibel seul ou assemblé, du fruité au plus concentré boisé.
DE: Nearly half of Spain's wine comes from Castilla-La Mancha, the vast central Meseta south of Madrid. La Mancha is Spain's largest DO; Airén, the most planted, gives neutral fresh whites but is declining as authorities encourage Tempranillo (Cencibel) and international varieties such as Cabernet, Merlot, Syrah, Chardonnay and Sauvignon. Major investment in technology and expertise makes it an excellent source of well-made inexpensive wines for export; the pago trend also began here. Valdepeñas, just south, has the same climate but a higher quality reputation, with Cencibel varietal or blended, from fruity to more concentrated oaked styles.
