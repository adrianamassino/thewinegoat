DE: Grenache is usually destemmed and crushed; pre-fermentation maceration is common to extract flavour and colour before alcohol rises. Some retain a proportion of whole bunches to enhance ripe red fruit. It is fermented in open-top vessels (stainless steel, cement or old oak) with gentle cap management such as punching down for more finesse. Post-fermentation maceration is used only if more tannic structure is wanted; usually the wine is drained off the skins at the end of fermentation and matured in large old oak.
