DE: White grapes remain a minority in the Southern Rhône, with no equivalent to Condrieu. Clairette, Grenache Blanc and Bourboulenc join the northern trio (Viognier, Marsanne, Roussanne). The best whites are usually richly textured and full-bodied, high in alcohol, with low to medium acidity and subtle fruity aromas; new oak is rarely used. Châteauneuf-du-Pape also makes a small amount of white.
