DE: Jerez has a hot, sunny Mediterranean climate; coastal vineyards are slightly cooler and benefit from the cool, humid westerly poniente, while the hot, drying easterly levante can stress vines. Being coastal, annual rain is high for Spain, but growing-season rain is limited: albariza soil lets the vine cope. Very chalky, it drains well yet its depth and excellent water-holding capacity store enough water for summer. In autumn and winter rectangular pits are dug between rows to trap water and reduce run-off; in summer albariza forms a hard crust limiting evaporation.
