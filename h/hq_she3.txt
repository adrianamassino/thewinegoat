Because of this systematic blending only average age can be stated. Each criadera and solera belongs to one system, and to reduce the risk of losing everything in one catastrophe, criaderas of the same system are often kept in different buildings.

Les vins peu collés et filtrés sont souvent étiquetés « en rama ».
DE: The solera's advantage is consistent style and quality. Some Sherries come from one solera system, but that is rare: most, including many of the best, blend wines from several. For very old Oloroso, blending can combine the intense flavours of very old wines, unbalanced alone, with the freshness of younger wines. Large producers own many solera systems with wines of different ages and styles for their blends. Most Sherry is fined and filtered before bottling, but there is a growing trend to minimise this, especially for biological wines whose vibrancy can be dulled. Wines with minimal fining and filtering are commonly labelled 'en rama'.
