DE: Yield is measured by weight (tonnes of grapes per hectare) or volume (hectolitres of wine per hectare). Producers must manage and predict it: to meet legal requirements such as in the EU, contractual obligations, or simply so the winemaker knows how much tank space is needed. Estimates start from buds left after pruning, but frost, poor fruit set, pests and diseases can dramatically reduce the final figure.

Q: Oltre a mangiare l'uva, quale altro danno causano uccelli e mammiferi, e come si proteggono le viti dai mammiferi? || En plus de manger le raisin, quel autre dégât causent oiseaux et mammifères, et comment protéger les vignes des mammifères ? || Besides eating grapes, what other harm do birds and mammals cause, and how are vines protected from mammals?

DI: Uccelli e mammiferi possono consumare grandi quantità di uva; inoltre gli acini mezzi mangiati o schiacciati aumentano il rischio di malattie fungine. Contro gli uccelli la soluzione migliore è spesso la rete, mentre contro mammiferi come conigli, cervi o cinghiali possono servire recinzioni protettive. DF: Oiseaux et mammifères peuvent consommer beaucoup de raisin ; de plus, les baies entamées ou écrasées augmentent le risque de maladies fongiques. Contre les oiseaux, les filets sont souvent la meilleure solution ; contre lapins, cerfs ou sangliers, des clôtures peuvent être nécessaires. DE: Birds and mammals can consume large volumes of grapes; half-eaten or crushed grapes also raise the risk of fungal disease. Netting is often best against birds, while protective fencing may be needed against mammals such as rabbits, deer or wild boar. Against insects, growers use insecticides or greener techniques such as integrated pest management.

DE: Pests and diseases may cut yield and/or quality: grapes can be lost or damaged by disease or hungry animals. Leaf damage reduces photosynthesis and limits the vine's ability to ripen its grapes. Some pests and diseases can be controlled; others may kill the vine.
