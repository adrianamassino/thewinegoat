#deg p2-9
Q: Spiega come valutare la qualità di un vino con il metodo SAT. || Expliquez comment évaluer la qualité d'un vin avec la méthode SAT. || Explain how to assess the quality of a wine using the SAT.
P6: Quali sono i quattro criteri (BLIC) e che cosa significa ciascuno? || Quels sont les quatre critères (BLIC) et que signifie chacun ? || What are the four criteria (BLIC) and what does each mean?
K: Balance: fruit/sweetness vs acidity/tannin/alcohol in harmony, none dominating; Length: how long pleasant flavours persist after swallowing/spitting (short to long); Intensity: of aromas/flavours, more pronounced generally better if not out of balance; Complexity: range/number of different aromas and flavours (primary, secondary, tertiary); each criterion met raises quality level; conclusion levels: faulty, poor, acceptable, good, very good, outstanding
P4: Come si usano per arrivare al giudizio di qualità e come si valuta la prontezza (readiness for drinking)? || Comment s'en sert-on pour conclure sur la qualité et comment juge-t-on l'aptitude au vieillissement ? || How are they used to reach a quality conclusion, and how is readiness for drinking judged?
K: count how many of BLIC are present/how strongly → outstanding has all four to a high degree; poor/acceptable lack several; readiness: too young / can drink now but has potential for ageing / drink now not suitable for ageing / too old; potential needs balance plus enough structure (acid, tannin, fruit concentration) and complexity that can develop; simple fruity wines drink now

#deg p4-7
Q: Il SAT descrive aspetto, naso e palato. || La SAT décrit l'aspect, le nez et la bouche. || The SAT describes appearance, nose and palate.
P4: Che cosa può indicare il colore di un vino bianco e di un vino rosso riguardo a età e vinificazione? || Que peut indiquer la couleur d'un blanc et d'un rouge sur l'âge et la vinification ? || What can colour indicate about age and winemaking for white and red wines?
K: whites gain colour with age (lemon → gold → amber) and from oak/oxidation; reds lose colour with age (purple → ruby → garnet → tawny); deep colour from thick skins, extraction, warm climate; rim/core difference shows age; amber/brown can indicate oxidation or deliberate oxidative ageing
P6: Distingui aromi primari, secondari e terziari con due esempi ciascuno. || Distinguez arômes primaires, secondaires et tertiaires avec deux exemples chacun. || Distinguish primary, secondary and tertiary aromas with two examples each.
K: primary from grape and alcoholic fermentation (floral, green/citrus/stone/tropical/red/black fruit, herbaceous, herbal, spice like pepper); secondary from post-fermentation winemaking: yeast/autolysis (biscuit, bread, lees), MLF (butter, cream), oak (vanilla, toast, cedar, smoke, coconut); tertiary from ageing: oxidative (almond, hazelnut, caramel, coffee) or bottle age (honey, petrol, mushroom, leather, earth, dried fruit)

#cib p10-13
Q: Abbinamento cibo-vino secondo il manuale. || Accords mets-vins selon le manuel. || Food and wine pairing according to the textbook.
P6: Spiega come dolcezza, umami, acidità e sale nel cibo modificano la percezione del vino. || Expliquez comment sucre, umami, acidité et sel dans le plat modifient la perception du vin. || Explain how sweetness, umami, acidity and salt in food change how a wine tastes.
K: sweetness and umami in food increase perception of bitterness, astringency, acidity and burning alcohol, decrease body, sweetness and fruitiness (make wine harder); acidity and salt in food increase body and fruit/sweetness, decrease bitterness, astringency and acidity (make wine softer); sweet food needs wine at least as sweet; umami → avoid tannic reds, add salt/acid to food
P4: Come interagiscono piccante (chili), grasso/olio e intensità aromatica con il vino? || Comment interagissent piment, gras/huile et intensité aromatique avec le vin ? || How do chilli heat, fat/oil and flavour intensity interact with wine?
K: chilli heat increases burning effect of alcohol and bitterness/astringency/acidity, reduces body/fruit/sweetness; choose low alcohol, off-dry, fruity, low tannin wines; fat/oil: high acidity or tannin cut through and refresh; flavour intensity of wine and food should match so neither overpowers; personal preferences/high-risk vs low-risk wines

#srv p14-16
Q: Conservazione e servizio del vino. || Conservation et service du vin. || Wine storage and service.
P4: Quali condizioni di conservazione a lungo termine raccomanda il libro e perché? || Quelles conditions de conservation longue recommande le livre et pourquoi ? || What long-term storage conditions does the book recommend and why?
K: cool constant temperature (around 10–15°C) — heat speeds ageing, fluctuations push wine past cork; away from light (UV damages wine, light strike); bottles with cork stored on their side so cork stays moist and does not dry/shrink letting oxygen in; away from strong odours and vibration; moderate humidity
P6: Spiega le temperature di servizio per diversi stili e come affrontare il servizio di uno spumante, un rosso con deposito e un vino difettoso. || Expliquez les températures de service selon les styles et comment servir un effervescent, un rouge à dépôt et un vin défectueux. || Explain serving temperatures for different styles and how to handle a sparkling wine, a red with sediment and a faulty wine.
K: sparkling well chilled 6–10°C; light/medium whites/rosés/sweet chilled 7–10°C; medium/full oaked whites lightly chilled 10–13°C; light reds lightly chilled 13°C; medium/full reds room temperature 15–18°C (too warm → alcohol, too cold → tannin harsh); sparkling: chill, hold cork, ease out with hiss not pop, tilt bottle, pour in two stages; sediment: stand upright, decant slowly against light, stop when sediment reaches neck; faults: cork taint (TCA, musty damp cardboard), oxidation, reduction, heat damage, Brett, VA — return/replace

#vig p18-21
Q: La vite e il suo ciclo annuale. || La vigne et son cycle annuel. || The vine and its annual cycle.
P5: Descrivi le fasi del ciclo annuale della vite dal germogliamento alla vendemmia. || Décrivez les étapes du cycle annuel de la vigne du débourrement à la vendange. || Describe the stages of the vine's annual cycle from budburst to harvest.
K: dormancy in winter (below ~10°C); budburst in spring when temperatures rise above ~10°C; shoot and leaf growth; flowering and fruit set (early summer, needs warm dry settled weather; poor weather → coulure/millerandage); grape development: green hard berries with high acid; véraison (colour change, softening, sugar accumulation begins); ripening: sugar up, acid down, tannins ripen, flavours develop, colour; harvest
P5: Come cambiano zuccheri, acidi, tannini e aromi durante la maturazione e quali rischi climatici incombono in primavera? || Comment évoluent sucres, acides, tanins et arômes pendant la maturation et quels risques climatiques menacent au printemps ? || How do sugars, acids, tannins and flavours change during ripening, and what climatic risks threaten in spring?
K: sugars (glucose, fructose) increase through photosynthesis; acidity (malic especially) falls, faster in warm climates; tannins and colour develop and mature, become less bitter/astringent; aroma compounds develop from green/herbaceous to riper fruit; spring frost can kill young shoots after budburst (early-budding varieties at risk); poor weather at flowering reduces fruit set

#vig p20-23
Q: Vitis vinifera, specie americane e innesti. || Vitis vinifera, espèces américaines et greffage. || Vitis vinifera, American species and grafting.
P5: Che cos'è la fillossera e perché la maggior parte delle viti vinifera è innestata? || Qu'est-ce que le phylloxéra et pourquoi la plupart des vinifera sont-elles greffées ? || What is phylloxera, and why are most vinifera vines grafted?
K: phylloxera is an aphid-like insect that feeds on vine roots, causing deformities/infections that kill vinifera; native American species evolved resistance; grafting vinifera scion onto American (or hybrid of American species) rootstock resists phylloxera; no cure; few areas phylloxera-free (e.g. Chile, parts of Australia) can grow own-rooted vines
P5: Oltre alla fillossera, perché si sceglie un portinnesto e che cosa sono incroci, ibridi e cloni? || En plus du phylloxéra, pourquoi choisit-on un porte-greffe et que sont croisements, hybrides et clones ? || Besides phylloxera, why choose a particular rootstock, and what are crossings, hybrids and clones?
K: rootstocks also chosen for nematode resistance, tolerance of drought/wet/lime/salt soils, controlling vigour (high or low); crossing = two varieties of the same species (e.g. vinifera × vinifera: Pinotage, Müller-Thurgau); hybrid = varieties of different species (e.g. Vidal), often frost/disease resistant; clone = vines propagated by cuttings from one parent, selected for traits such as yield, disease resistance, flavour, ripening time

#env p24-31
Q: Ambiente del vigneto: clima e fattori naturali. || Environnement du vignoble : climat et facteurs naturels. || Vineyard environment: climate and natural factors.
P6: Quali sono i bisogni della vite (calore, luce, acqua, nutrienti) e come influiscono latitudine e altitudine? || Quels sont les besoins de la vigne (chaleur, lumière, eau, nutriments) et quel est l'effet de la latitude et de l'altitude ? || What does the vine need (heat, sunlight, water, nutrients), and how do latitude and altitude affect it?
K: heat: vine needs ~10°C+ to grow, warmth to ripen; sunlight for photosynthesis (sugar); water: too little → stress, closes stomata, stops ripening; too much → vigour/dilution/disease; nutrients (nitrogen etc.), moderate amounts; latitude: most vineyards 30–50° N/S; nearer equator hotter; higher latitudes longer summer days; altitude: temperature falls ~0.6°C per 100 m, cooler sites in hot regions (e.g. Salta, Andes), higher UV, wider diurnal range
P6: Spiega l'effetto di oceani/correnti, montagne, esposizione, nebbia, suolo e venti sul clima del vigneto. || Expliquez l'effet des océans/courants, montagnes, exposition, brouillard, sol et vents sur le climat du vignoble. || Explain the effect of oceans/currents, mountains, aspect, fog, soil and wind on vineyard climate.
K: oceans moderate temperatures (smaller annual range); warm currents (Gulf Stream) warm, cold currents (Humboldt, Benguela, California) cool coasts; mountains create rain shadow and shelter (Vosges, Andes), slopes; aspect: slopes facing the equator receive more sunlight, steep slopes better angle, cold air drains away from slopes (frost); fog cools and reduces sunlight; soil: water drainage and retention, nutrients, heat retention by stones (Châteauneuf galets); winds cool (Mistral) or warm, can damage, reduce disease

#env p28-30
Q: Clima, condizioni meteo e annata. || Climat, conditions météo et millésime. || Climate, weather and vintage.
P4: Distingui clima continentale, marittimo e mediterraneo e le classi fresco/moderato/caldo. || Distinguez climats continental, maritime et méditerranéen et les catégories frais/modéré/chaud. || Distinguish continental, maritime and Mediterranean climates and cool/moderate/hot classes.
K: continental: large annual temperature range, short summers, cold winters, spring frost risk (Burgundy, Champagne); maritime: low annual range, mild winters, cool summers, rain spread through year (Bordeaux); Mediterranean: low annual range, warm dry summers, rain in winter (Southern France); cool <16.5°C, moderate 16.5–18.5°C, warm 18.5–21°C, hot >21°C average growing-season temperature
P6: Quali eventi meteo possono danneggiare un'annata e come rispondono i viticoltori? || Quels événements météo peuvent abîmer un millésime et comment répondent les vignerons ? || What weather events can damage a vintage, and how do growers respond?
K: winter freeze (bury vines, choose hardy varieties, hybrids); spring frost (sprinklers, heaters, wind machines, late pruning, site selection on slopes); poor weather at flowering (coulure, millerandage → low yield); hail (nets, insurance, scattered vineyards); drought (irrigation where allowed, drought-tolerant rootstocks); excess rain (rot, dilution, swollen berries); heat waves (sunburn, stops ripening; canopy shade); fire smoke taint; vintage variation greater in marginal climates

#vgm p32-37
Q: Gestione del vigneto: forme di allevamento e potatura. || Conduite du vignoble : conduite et taille. || Vineyard management: training and pruning.
P6: Confronta potatura a sperone (cordone) e a capo (Guyot), alberello e spalliera, e perché si scelgono. || Comparez taille à coursons (cordon) et à long bois (Guyot), gobelet et palissage, et pourquoi on les choisit. || Compare spur pruning (cordon) and replacement cane pruning (Guyot), bush vines and trellised vines, and why each is chosen.
K: spur pruning: permanent cordon with short spurs of 2–3 buds; easier, suits mechanisation; replacement cane: one or two canes of 8–20 buds tied to wire each year, more skilled, can reduce risk where buds less fertile; bush vines (gobelet): head-trained, no trellis, low vigour, warm dry sunny regions, cheap but no mechanisation, fruit shaded; trellised (VSP): shoots trained up wires, good sun exposure and airflow, mechanisation, cost
P4: Che cosa sono vigore, gestione della chioma e densità d'impianto e come incidono su resa e qualità? || Que sont vigueur, gestion du feuillage et densité de plantation et quel est leur effet sur rendement et qualité ? || What are vigour, canopy management and planting density, and how do they affect yield and quality?
K: vigour = vine's tendency to grow shoots/leaves; excess vigour shades fruit, raises disease risk, diverts energy from grapes; canopy management: positioning shoots, leaf removal, summer pruning, green harvest to balance; density: high density (Bordeaux, Burgundy ~10,000 vines/ha) in poor soils makes vines compete, lower yield per vine, quality; low density in dry warm areas where water scarce; balanced vine optimises yield and ripeness

#vgm p36-41
Q: Rischi del vigneto, vendemmia e scelte di coltivazione. || Risques au vignoble, vendanges et choix de culture. || Vineyard hazards, harvest and growing choices.
P6: Descrivi parassiti e malattie principali (oidio, peronospora, botrytis, virus) e i mezzi di controllo. || Décrivez parasites et maladies principaux (oïdium, mildiou, botrytis, virus) et les moyens de lutte. || Describe the main pests and diseases (powdery mildew, downy mildew, botrytis, viruses) and their controls.
K: powdery mildew (oidium): fungus on green parts, warm damp shady, sulfur sprays; downy mildew: humid warm wet, copper (Bordeaux mixture); botrytis bunch rot: humid conditions, damaged berries, grey rot spoils, canopy airflow, fungicides; noble rot positive form for sweet wines; viruses: spread by insects/nematodes or cuttings, no cure, use healthy stock, remove vines; pests: birds (nets), animals, insects, nematodes; integrated pest management, organic (copper/sulfur only), biodynamic
P4: Vendemmia manuale o meccanica: vantaggi e svantaggi. || Vendange manuelle ou mécanique : avantages et inconvénients. || Hand versus machine harvesting: advantages and disadvantages.
K: hand: selective (remove rotten/unripe bunches), whole bunches intact (sparkling, whole-bunch fermentation, noble rot successive tries), steep slopes; slow, expensive, labour availability; machine: fast, cheap, can pick at night when cool, at optimum moment; damages fruit (oxidation risk), not selective, needs trellis and accessible flat land, cannot do whole bunches

#vin p42-47
Q: Vinificazione: principi di base. || Vinification : principes de base. || Winemaking: basic principles.
P6: Che cosa serve per la fermentazione alcolica e come incidono temperatura, lieviti e ossigeno? || Que faut-il pour la fermentation alcoolique et quel est l'effet de la température, des levures et de l'oxygène ? || What does alcoholic fermentation need, and what is the effect of temperature, yeast and oxygen?
K: sugar + yeast → alcohol + CO2 + heat; yeast needs nutrients, suitable temperature; too hot (>35°C) kills yeast/stuck fermentation, too cold slows; cool for whites (12–22°C) keeps fruity aromas; warmer for reds (20–32°C) extracts colour/tannin; ambient (wild) yeasts complexity/risk vs cultured yeasts predictable; anaerobic winemaking (inert vessels, inert gas, SO2) preserves primary fruit; controlled oxygen softens, develops tertiary
P6: Spiega l'uso di SO2, l'aggiustamento di zucchero e acidità e il ruolo dei recipienti (acciaio, cemento, legno). || Expliquez l'usage du SO2, l'ajustement du sucre et de l'acidité et le rôle des contenants (inox, béton, bois). || Explain the use of SO2, sugar and acid adjustments and the role of vessels (stainless steel, concrete, oak).
K: SO2 antioxidant and antiseptic, added at crushing/maturation/bottling; too much → aroma; enrichment/chaptalisation (add sugar or concentrated must) to raise alcohol in cool climates, limited by law; acidification (tartaric acid) in warm climates; deacidification in cool; stainless steel inert, easy temperature control, cleaning; concrete inert or lined, stable temperature; oak: oxygen exposure softens tannin, adds flavours (vanilla, toast, clove) more with new/small/toasted barrels; French vs American oak

#vin p46-49
Q: Maturazione, chiarifica e imbottigliamento. || Élevage, clarification et mise en bouteille. || Maturation, clarification and bottling.
P5: Confronta chiarifica (fining), filtrazione e stabilizzazione tartarica. || Comparez collage, filtration et stabilisation tartrique. || Compare fining, filtration and tartrate stabilisation.
K: clarification by sedimentation/racking, centrifuge; fining agents (bentonite, egg white, gelatin, casein, isinglass) remove proteins/tannins/haze by binding; depth filtration (diatomaceous earth) for heavy loads; surface/membrane filtration sterile fine; too much can strip flavour/body; tartrate stabilisation by cold stabilisation (chill to −4°C to precipitate crystals) to avoid crystals in bottle
P5: Che cosa cambia nel vino con l'affinamento in botte rispetto all'acciaio e quali chiusure esistono con i loro pro e contro? || Qu'apporte l'élevage en fût par rapport à l'inox et quelles fermetures existent avec leurs avantages et inconvénients ? || What does barrel maturation add compared with stainless steel, and which closures exist with their pros and cons?
K: oak: slow oxygen softens tannins, stabilises colour, adds oak flavours (new more than old, small more than large, toast level), evaporation concentrates; steel/inert preserves fresh primary fruit; lees ageing adds body/biscuit; natural cork: ages wine, risk of TCA, variable oxygen; synthetic: cheap, no TCA, short-term; screwcap: no TCA, consistent, preserves fruit, can lead to reduction; glass stoppers etc.

#wht p50-53
Q: Vinificazione dei vini bianchi. || Vinification des vins blancs. || White winemaking.
P6: Spiega le scelte per un bianco fruttato aromatico (es. Sauvignon) rispetto a un bianco complesso e ricco (es. Chardonnay in legno). || Expliquez les choix pour un blanc fruité aromatique (ex. sauvignon) face à un blanc riche et complexe (ex. chardonnay boisé). || Explain the choices for a fruity aromatic white (e.g. Sauvignon) versus a rich, complex white (e.g. oaked Chardonnay).
K: aromatic: protective/anaerobic handling, rapid pressing, possibly short skin contact, cool fermentation in stainless steel, cultured aromatic yeast, no MLF, no oak, early bottling; complex: barrel fermentation (integrates oak), MLF (butter, softer acid), lees contact and stirring (bâtonnage) for texture and biscuit/bread, maturation in oak (new %), ambient yeasts, controlled oxygen
P4: Quali tecniche si usano per i bianchi dolci (fermentazione arrestata, aggiunta di mosto, concentrazione)? || Quelles techniques pour les blancs doux (arrêt de fermentation, ajout de moût, concentration) ? || What techniques produce sweet white wines (stopping fermentation, adding sweet reserve, concentration)?
K: stop fermentation by chilling and filtering/SO2, or fortification; blending with sweet reserve (unfermented juice, Süssreserve) after fermentation; concentrate sugar in grapes: noble rot (Sauternes, Tokaji), drying grapes (passito), freezing on vine (Icewine/Eiswein); high-sugar juice ferments slowly, stops naturally with sugar left

#red p54-60
Q: Vinificazione dei vini rossi. || Vinification des vins rouges. || Red winemaking.
P6: Quali tecniche di estrazione esistono durante la fermentazione e come le si sceglie in base allo stile? || Quelles techniques d'extraction existent pendant la fermentation et comment les choisir selon le style ? || What extraction techniques exist during fermentation, and how are they chosen for style?
K: cap of skins rises; punching down (pigeage) gentle-moderate; pumping over (remontage) aeration, temperature; rack and return (délestage) gentle but thorough; rotary fermenters fast; extraction depends on temperature (warmer more), time on skins, intensity; pre-fermentation cold soak for colour/fruit; post-fermentation maceration for softer, finer tannins; light fruity → less extraction, cooler; full tannic → more extraction, warmer, longer maceration
P6: Spiega macerazione carbonica e fermentazione a grappolo intero, la pressatura (vin de goutte/vin de presse) e la produzione di rosati. || Expliquez macération carbonique et fermentation en grappes entières, le pressurage (vin de goutte/vin de presse) et la production de rosés. || Explain carbonic maceration and whole-bunch fermentation, pressing (free-run/press wine) and rosé production.
K: carbonic: whole bunches in CO2-filled sealed vat, intracellular fermentation, then pressing; bright colour, low tannin, red cherry, kirsch, banana, bubblegum, cinnamon (Beaujolais); semi-carbonic: crushed grapes at bottom; whole bunch in Burgundy/Rhône adds fruit and stem tannin/fresh herbal; free-run wine drains off; press wine darker, more tannic, can be blended to adjust; rosé: short maceration (direct/saignée), direct pressing of black grapes (pale), blending red and white (rarely allowed in EU except Champagne)

#prx p62-71
Q: Prezzo e qualità del vino. || Prix et qualité du vin. || Wine price and quality.
P6: Quali costi di vigneto e di cantina spiegano la differenza di prezzo tra un vino economico e uno premium? || Quels coûts au vignoble et à la cave expliquent l'écart de prix entre un vin bon marché et un haut de gamme ? || Which vineyard and winery costs explain the price difference between an inexpensive and a premium wine?
K: land price (prestigious regions), planting/establishment; yields (low yields cost more per bottle); labour (hand work on steep slopes, hand harvesting); machinery vs manual; grape purchase price; winery equipment; oak barrels (new oak expensive), time in barrel/bottle ties up capital and storage; packaging (bottle, closure, label); economies of scale for large producers; marketing, transport, taxes and duty (fixed per bottle → bigger share for cheap wines), distributor/retailer margins; supply and demand, reputation, rarity
P4: Che cosa sono le GI europee (DOP/IGP) e quali regole stabiliscono le denominazioni? || Que sont les IG européennes (AOP/IGP) et quelles règles fixent les appellations ? || What are EU GIs (PDO/PGI) and what rules do appellations set?
K: PDO (AOC/AOP, DOC/DOCG, DO/DOCa): smaller areas, stricter rules: permitted varieties, viticulture (yields, training, density), winemaking, ageing, minimum alcohol; PGI (IGP, IGT, VdlT): larger areas, fewer restrictions, more freedom of varieties; wine without GI (Vin de France) can show variety and vintage; non-EU GIs (AVA etc.) only define boundaries; labelling terms (Reserva etc.)

#prx p66-69
Q: Leggi sul vino ed etichetta. || Lois sur le vin et étiquetage. || Wine law and labelling.
P5: Quali termini d'etichetta regolati per legge (es. Riserva, Gran Reserva, Prädikat) e non regolati (es. Old Vines, Reserve fuori UE) conosci, e perché la differenza conta? || Quels termes d'étiquette réglementés (ex. Riserva, Gran Reserva, Prädikat) et non réglementés (ex. Old Vines, Reserve hors UE) connaissez-vous, et pourquoi la différence compte-t-elle ? || Which regulated labelling terms (e.g. Riserva, Gran Reserva, Prädikat) and unregulated ones (e.g. Old Vines, Reserve outside the EU) do you know, and why does the difference matter?
K: regulated: Spanish Crianza/Reserva/Gran Reserva minimum ageing; Italian Riserva; German Prädikat by must weight; Grand Cru/Premier Cru in Burgundy; Classico; Vintage; varietal labelling rules (85% varietal/vintage in EU); unregulated: old vines/vieilles vignes, reserve/reserva in New World, single vineyard in some countries, barrel select; regulated terms guarantee minimum standards, unregulated rely on producer reputation
P5: In che modo le regole sulle rese e sulle varietà possono migliorare o limitare la qualità? || En quoi les règles de rendement et de cépages peuvent-elles améliorer ou limiter la qualité ? || How can rules on yields and varieties improve or limit quality?
K: maximum yields aim at concentration, protect reputation; permitted varieties ensure typicity; but restrictions limit innovation; producers may declassify to IGP/Vin de France (e.g. Super Tuscans used international varieties as Vino da Tavola) for freedom; climate change may require new varieties; rules do not guarantee quality of every producer
