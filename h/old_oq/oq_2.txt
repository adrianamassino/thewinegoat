P6: Spiega perché il Cabernet Sauvignon domina nel Médoc e il Merlot a Saint-Émilion e Pomerol. || Expliquez pourquoi le cabernet-sauvignon domine dans le Médoc et le merlot à Saint-Émilion et Pomerol. || Explain why Cabernet Sauvignon dominates the Médoc and Merlot dominates Saint-Émilion and Pomerol.
K: Médoc/Graves: warm gravel soils (heat retention, drainage) let late-ripening Cabernet ripen; Gironde estuary moderates; maritime climate, Gulf Stream; Right Bank: cooler, damper clay and limestone soils (water retentive) suit earlier ripening Merlot, which ripens reliably in cooler soils; Cabernet Franc also important on Right Bank (Saint-Émilion); Pomerol clay with iron pan; Merlot gives plump fruit, softer tannins
P6: Perché il taglio è fondamentale a Bordeaux e che ruolo hanno Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc e Petit Verdot? || Pourquoi l'assemblage est-il essentiel à Bordeaux et quel rôle jouent cabernet-sauvignon, merlot, cabernet franc et petit verdot ? || Why is blending essential in Bordeaux, and what do Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot contribute?
K: maritime climate, vintage variation and rain (flowering, harvest) risk: varieties flower and ripen at different times, spreading risk; Cabernet Sauvignon: colour, high tannin, acidity, blackcurrant, structure for ageing, late ripening; Merlot: body, softness, plum, earlier ripening, alcohol; Cabernet Franc: fragrance, red fruit, leafy; Petit Verdot: colour, tannin, spice in small amounts, ripens late; Malbec small; blend gives balance and complexity

P5: Descrivi la differenza tra un Bordeaux AC di base e un Grand Cru Classé del Médoc in vigneto e in cantina. || Décrivez la différence entre un Bordeaux AC basique et un grand cru classé du Médoc à la vigne et à la cave. || Describe the difference between a basic Bordeaux AC and a Médoc Grand Cru Classé in the vineyard and winery.
K: basic: from anywhere in Bordeaux (often Entre-Deux-Mers), Merlot-dominant, higher yields, machine harvest, little or no new oak, fruity, drink young; Classé: best gravel sites, low yields, hand harvest, sorting, careful extraction, new oak barrique maturation 18–24 months, second wines, full-bodied, high tannin, long ageing with cedar, tobacco, forest floor; 1855 classification (five growths)
P5: Come si producono i bianchi secchi di Pessac-Léognan e i dolci di Sauternes? || Comment produit-on les blancs secs de Pessac-Léognan et les liquoreux de Sauternes ? || How are dry Pessac-Léognan whites and sweet Sauternes made?
K: dry whites: Sauvignon Blanc (fresh, aromatic) and Sémillon (body, ageing, lanolin); Pessac-Léognan best barrel-fermented and aged with lees, complex, age to toast/honey; Entre-Deux-Mers fresh unoaked; Sauternes/Barsac: Sémillon (thin skins, prone to noble rot) with Sauvignon; autumn mists from Ciron river meeting Garonne + warm afternoons → noble rot; successive hand picking (tries); fermentation in barrels, new oak; luscious, high acidity, honey, apricot, marmalade; risky and costly

K: less famous, lower land prices and less demand; similar varieties and climate to Bordeaux (inland is warmer/more continental); IGP Côtes de Gascogne large-volume fresh whites from varieties once used for Armagnac distillation; local varieties distinctive; modern winemaking

P6: Descrivi il clima e i fattori di sito della Côte d'Or e perché i migliori vigneti sono a mezza costa. || Décrivez le climat et les facteurs de site de la Côte d'Or et pourquoi les meilleures vignes sont à mi-pente. || Describe the Côte d'Or's climate and site factors, and why the best vineyards lie mid-slope.
K: cool continental climate, cold winters, spring frost, hail, rain at flowering/harvest; vintage variation; east/south-east facing slope catches morning sun; mid-slope: limestone and marl soils with good drainage, best sun exposure, away from frost at bottom and exposure/thin soils at top; flat land below (village/regional AC); sheltered by hills to the west; marginal ripening for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay
P6: Spiega la gerarchia delle AC di Borgogna e il ruolo dei négociants rispetto ai domaines. || Expliquez la hiérarchie des AC de Bourgogne et le rôle des négociants face aux domaines. || Explain Burgundy's AC hierarchy and the role of négociants versus domaines.
K: regional (Bourgogne, Bourgogne Hautes-Côtes), village (Gevrey-Chambertin, Meursault), Premier Cru (village + vineyard), Grand Cru (vineyard name alone, e.g. Chambertin, Montrachet); about 1% grand cru; vineyards fragmented by Napoleonic inheritance law — many owners of small plots (monopoles rare); négociants buy grapes/must/wine and blend, large volume, some own vineyards; domaines grow and make their own; quality varies by producer

K: Chablis: coolest, Kimmeridgian clay-limestone, frost (sprinklers, heaters), green apple, lemon, high acidity, usually little or no new oak; Grand Cru slope fuller, sometimes oaked; Petit Chablis; Côte de Beaune (Meursault, Puligny, Chassagne): barrel fermentation, lees, MLF, new oak, fuller, complex, nutty, ageing; Mâconnais: warmer, riper, fuller, melon, citrus, often unoaked; Pouilly-Fuissé richer, oaked
P4: Perché il Gamay e la macerazione carbonica caratterizzano il Beaujolais, e che cosa distingue i Cru? || Pourquoi le gamay et la macération carbonique caractérisent-ils le Beaujolais, et qu'est-ce qui distingue les crus ? || Why do Gamay and carbonic maceration characterise Beaujolais, and what distinguishes the Crus?
K: warmer than Burgundy, granite soils in north; Gamay: light-medium body, high acidity, low tannin, red fruit; carbonic/semi-carbonic gives bright fruit, kirsch, banana, low tannin, drink young (Beaujolais Nouveau released in November); Crus (10, e.g. Morgon, Moulin-à-Vent, Fleurie) on granite hills in the north, more concentration and structure, some traditional maceration and oak, can age

K: Alsace AC: usually varietal labelled, single variety 100%; Alsace Grand Cru: 51 named vineyards, lower yields, generally only the four noble grapes (with exceptions); VT: late-harvest grapes, higher must weight, may be dry or sweet, noble varieties; SGN: botrytised, very sweet, rare; sweetness level not always indicated on label; Crémant d'Alsace

P6: Spiega perché il Chenin Blanc dà stili così diversi in Vouvray, Savennières e Coteaux du Layon. || Expliquez pourquoi le chenin donne des styles si différents à Vouvray, Savennières et dans les Coteaux du Layon. || Explain why Chenin Blanc gives such different styles in Vouvray, Savennières and Coteaux du Layon.

K: Muscadet (Pays Nantais): Melon Blanc (Melon de Bourgogne), maritime climate near Atlantic, neutral, light, high acid; sur lie: aged on lees until bottling → creamy texture, yeasty notes, slight spritz; Sèvre et Maine; Sancerre/Pouilly-Fumé (Central Vineyards): Sauvignon Blanc, continental cool, high acid, green fruit, gooseberry, flinty/smoky (silex), mostly unoaked; Sancerre red/rosé Pinot Noir

K: Côtes du Rhône (regional, majority, Grenache-based), Côtes du Rhône Villages, CDR Villages + named village (e.g. Cairanne, Plan de Dieu), crus (Châteauneuf-du-Pape, Gigondas, Vacqueyras, Tavel rosé, Lirac); Châteauneuf: up to 13 permitted varieties (18 counted), Grenache dominant, low yields, galets (large round stones) store heat, full body, high alcohol, red fruit, spice, garrigue, ages; also whites
