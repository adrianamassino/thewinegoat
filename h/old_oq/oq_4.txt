K: Palomino from albariza (chalk, water retention); quick pressing, neutral dry base wine fermented 20–25°C, ~11–12%; first classification: pale, delicate wines for biological ageing; fortified to 15–15.5% (ideal for flor); sobretabla; second classification; solera system in 600 L butts filled five-sixths; flor (yeast) layer protects from oxygen, consumes alcohol and glycerol, produces acetaldehyde (bready, tangy, almond); needs cool humid conditions, nutrients from frequent topping up; criaderas; average age rarely > 3–4 years; Manzanilla in Sanlúcar; bottled pale lemon, dry, drink fresh, en rama
P4: Perché la solera dà un vino costante? || Pourquoi la solera donne-t-elle un vin constant ? || Why does the solera system give a consistent wine?
K: fractional blending: wine drawn from the oldest level (solera) in equal amounts from each butt, never fully emptied; replenished from the next criadera, blended, and so on; youngest topped with sobretabla wine; new wine takes on character of older wine; consistent style each year; only average age; requires good wine input and limited withdrawals
