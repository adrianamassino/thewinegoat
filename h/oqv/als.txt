Q: Alsazia. || Alsace. || Alsace.
P5: Perché l'Alsazia, così a nord, produce vini maturi e quali sono le quattro uve nobili e i loro stili? || Pourquoi l'Alsace, si au nord, produit-elle des vins mûrs et quels sont les quatre cépages nobles et leurs styles ? || Why does Alsace, so far north, produce ripe wines, and what are the four noble grapes and their styles?
K: cool to moderate continental; Vosges shelter from westerly rain-bearing winds → few clouds, sunny summers, dry autumns, high sugar ripeness (drought in driest years); best vineyards steepest slopes facing east/south-east, vines trained low for radiated heat; plain vines high against frost, for Crémant; northern part coolest; varied geology influences styles; low disease → organic/biodynamic; Riesling most planted noble: dry, medium-full body, medium alcohol, high acid, citrus, stone fruit, steely (less floral than German; more now with sugar); Gewurztraminer: pungent lychee, rose, sweet spice, pink skin → golden, full, oily, low-medium acid, high alcohol (14%); Pinot Gris: rich, full, high alcohol, more acid than Gewurz, less aromatic, fresh and dried fruit, honey; Muscat (best Muscat Blanc à Petits Grains, orange blossom, rose, grape, light-medium body, low-medium acid; Ottonel more planted as Petits Grains suffers poor set and rot)
P5: Spiega le diciture Alsace AC, Alsace Grand Cru, Vendanges Tardives e Sélection de Grains Nobles. || Expliquez Alsace AC, Alsace Grand Cru, Vendanges Tardives et Sélection de Grains Nobles. || Explain Alsace AC, Alsace Grand Cru, Vendanges Tardives and Sélection de Grains Nobles.
K: Alsace AC: vast majority, mostly single variety, varietal label means 100%; blends not varietally labelled (Edelzwicker, Gentil, brands; rare high-quality blends); some villages may add their name; Alsace Grand Cru: over 50 vineyards, single noble variety (few exceptions), label must state vineyard, vintage, variety; controversy over boundaries too large → some producers don't use it; VT: late harvest, one of four noble varieties, minimum sugar by variety, best with passerillage, some noble rot, can be dry to medium sweet; SGN: higher minimum sugar, usually noble rot, always sweet, not every year, small quantities; no official sweetness labelling → increasingly sweet grand crus confuse consumers, know house style; unofficial terms (Réserve personnelle, Cuvée spéciale) no legal status; long harvest mid-September to December for VT/SGN

Q: La Valle della Loira. || La vallée de la Loire. || The Loire Valley.
P6: Spiega perché il Chenin Blanc dà stili così diversi in Vouvray, Savennières e Coteaux du Layon. || Expliquez pourquoi le chenin donne des styles si différents à Vouvray, Savennières et dans les Coteaux du Layon. || Explain why Chenin Blanc gives such different styles in Vouvray, Savennières and Coteaux du Layon.
K: high acid non-aromatic; uneven ripening even within a bunch → several passes, unripe gives green herbaceous; style depends on ripeness: barely ripe (10–11%) for sparkling, increasingly ripe for dry, medium, sweet (shrivelled or noble rot); trend to riper dry wines with a few grams sugar; apple to tropical fruit, steely smoky dry wines, apricot/citrus peel noble rot; ages decades to honey, toast, hay; Vouvray (east of Tours, Touraine): cool, clay soils, still dry to lusciously sweet by vintage plus sparkling, light-medium body, fresh fruity floral, rarely new oak; Anjou/Saumur: Saumur traditional-method sparkling, Anjou dry often new oak; Savennières and Coteaux du Layon in warmer drier western Anjou (Mauges hills, stony soils) → medium-full, less floral; Savennières: well-exposed, air circulation impedes noble rot, late-harvested full dry wines that age for decades; Coteaux du Layon: sheltered Layon valley, noble rot, Quarts de Chaume and Bonnezeaux among world's greatest sweet wines
P4: Confronta Muscadet e Sancerre/Pouilly-Fumé. || Comparez le muscadet et Sancerre/Pouilly-Fumé. || Compare Muscadet and Sancerre/Pouilly-Fumé.
K: Muscadet (Nantais, maritime, near Atlantic): Melon Blanc ripens early, frost-resistant; Muscadet Sèvre et Maine largest production; dry, max 12% abv, high acidity, light body, subtle green fruit, drink young; concrete/steel (formerly old oak); sur lie: winter on lees, bottled in spring, richer texture, little handling; Sancerre/Pouilly-Fumé (Central Vineyards, continental, smallest production): Sauvignon on chalky stony well-drained soils; dry, high acid, green apple, wet stones, Pouilly-Fumé subtle smoky; Chavignol, Les Monts Damnés; mostly not for ageing; pneumatic pressing and steel for fresh styles, some old oak and lees for texture, a few new oak; MLF in cool vintages to lower acidity; Menetou-Salon cheaper similar; Touraine Sauvignon simpler

Q: Loira: rossi e rosati. || Loire : rouges et rosés. || Loire: reds and rosés.
P6: Descrivi i rossi di Cabernet Franc della Touraine (Chinon, Bourgueil, Saumur-Champigny) e i rosati d'Anjou. || Décrivez les rouges de cabernet franc de Touraine (Chinon, Bourgueil, Saumur-Champigny) et les rosés d'Anjou. || Describe the Cabernet Franc reds of Touraine (Chinon, Bourgueil, Saumur-Champigny) and the rosés of Anjou.
K: Cabernet Franc suits the Loire because it flowers and ripens early; Chinon (south of Loire), Bourgueil and Saint-Nicolas-de-Bourgueil (north): sandy soils → light fruity early drinking; south-facing limestone and clay slopes → fuller, more tannic; some oak, best evolve for years; Saumur and Saumur-Champigny lighter in body and tannin, juicy berry, floral, best young, can be chilled (some fuller producers); Gamay second black grape (Touraine, Anjou) fruity early; Pinot Noir small amounts in Central Vineyards, light; rosés: Rosé de Loire always dry, min 30% Cabernet Franc/Sauvignon, mainly Anjou-Saumur; Cabernet d'Anjou highest quality, always medium-sweet, Cabernet Franc + Sauvignon; Rosé d'Anjou less sweet, mainly Grolleau (high-yielding local) with Cabernet Franc; Touraine rosés dry fresh from many grapes; Sancerre rosé Pinot Noir, pale, dry, delicate; direct pressing or short maceration, inert temperature-controlled fermentation
P4: Come sono organizzate le sottoregioni della Loira e come variano i climi? || Comment s'organisent les sous-régions de la Loire et comment varient les climats ? || How are the Loire's sub-regions organised and how do climates vary?
K: most important vineyards along last few hundred km of France's longest river; four sub-regions east to west: Central Vineyards, Touraine, Anjou-Saumur, Nantais (not appellations); no regional generic appellation for whole Loire (Rosé de Loire, Crémant de Loire focused in Anjou/Saumur), IGP Val de Loire covers region: fresh, light, simple; overall cool; Central Vineyards continental, Nantais maritime; Anjou warmer, drier (Mauges hills, stony soils); Touraine cooler, wetter, clay; northern limit for its varieties → strong vintage variation, fungal disease; best sites south-facing mid-slope facing river for heat and reflected light
