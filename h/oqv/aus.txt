P6: Confronta lo Shiraz di Barossa Valley, Hunter Valley e Heathcote/zone fresche. || Comparez la shiraz de Barossa Valley, Hunter Valley et Heathcote/zones fraîches. || Compare Shiraz from Barossa Valley, Hunter Valley and Heathcote/cooler areas.

P6: Come sono il Riesling di Clare/Eden e il Semillon della Hunter, e perché invecchiano? || Comment sont le riesling de Clare/Eden et le sémillon de Hunter, et pourquoi vieillissent-ils ? || What are Clare/Eden Riesling and Hunter Semillon like, and why do they age?
K: Riesling unique Australian style: pronounced citrus (lime, lemon, grapefruit) young, develops rapidly into toast, honey, petrol; unoaked, high acidity, dry or slightly off-dry (some sweet); Clare Valley: warm tempered by cool afternoon breezes, cold nights, altitude 300–400 m (up to 570 m) → dry, intense citrus/lime, high acid, honey and toast with age; Eden Valley: hills east of Barossa, cool-moderate with altitude; intense lime, grapefruit, steely; best show marmalade and toast after ten years; Tasmania, Frankland River less citrusy; Hunter Semillon unique: harvested early, low sugar, high acidity; minimal oxygen contact, inert vessels for fermentation and storage; light alcohol, almost neutral when bottled; develops extraordinary honey, toast (nut) with bottle age; best 20 years or more; WA herbaceous Semillon (like Sauvignon); Barossa traditionally fuller softer, now early-picked unoaked

K: isolated, 1900 km from Australia → maritime; South Island cool, North a little warmer; long sunshine hours, nights cooled by sea breezes, long ripening period → high sugar and flavour while retaining acidity; South Island vineyards on east side, protected from westerly rain by mountains, but plentiful rain can be a problem; free-draining soils, flat land fertile → vigour → expertise in trellising/canopy management; Sauvignon flagship since early 1990s, majority of production: pungent, elderflower, passion fruit, high acid, world benchmark; North more tropical, South higher acid, green bell pepper, gooseberry; fermented cool in inert vessels, some oaked as point of difference; Marlborough major centre, Wairau (by far largest; long sunny days like Martinborough; southern side valleys for aspect/altitude) and Awatere (drier, cooler, windier → higher acidity, pronounced herbaceous, lacks tropical notes)
P4: Confronta il Pinot Noir di Central Otago, Martinborough e Marlborough. || Comparez le pinot noir de Central Otago, Martinborough et Marlborough. || Compare Pinot Noir from Central Otago, Martinborough and Marlborough.
K: Pinot Noir second most planted; vibrant fruit, fine ripe tannins, soft texture, often quite high alcohol; Central Otago: inland foothills of Southern Alps, continental (unlike rest of NZ), frost risk spring and autumn, warm summers, large diurnal range, very high sunlight intensity → high alcohol; vast majority Pinot: full-bodied, juicy, vibrant, concentrated ripe red fruit; Martinborough (Wairarapa, southern tip of North Island): summer heat but wide diurnal range; medium-full, ripe, dark plum and spice; Marlborough: medium-bodied, fine tannins, cherry, cranberry; still and sparkling, increasingly impressive

P5: Che cosa caratterizza Chardonnay e varietà aromatiche neozelandesi e le regioni di Nelson e Canterbury? || Qu'est-ce qui caractérise chardonnay et cépages aromatiques néo-zélandais et les régions de Nelson et Canterbury ? || What characterises New Zealand Chardonnay and aromatic varieties, and the Nelson and Canterbury regions?
