Q: Austria. || Autriche. || Austria.
P6: Descrivi clima, regioni (Niederösterreich, Burgenland) e lo stile del Grüner Veltliner e del Riesling della Wachau. || Décrivez climat, régions (Niederösterreich, Burgenland) et le style du grüner veltliner et du riesling de Wachau. || Describe the climate, regions (Niederösterreich, Burgenland) and the style of Grüner Veltliner and Wachau Riesling.
K: Alps in west → vineyards in east; cool continental, short summers, very harsh winters; northern regions cooling northerly winds, near Hungary warmed by easterly winds; less annual variation than northern Europe; Grüner Veltliner most planted: high yields → fresh but unexciting, careful growing → full, concentrated, naturally high acid, citrus/stone fruit, white pepper, honey and toast with age; mostly stainless steel, old oak casks common, some new barriques; Riesling less planted, best from Wachau, Kamptal, Kremstal: dry, medium-full, ripe peachy, can age; Niederösterreich largest for production and exports, along Danube; Wachau steep terraced south-facing vineyards by Danube → concentrated; Wachau not a DAC, own classification; Kamptal and Kremstal DACs similar style; Weinviertel DAC largest and first, Grüner only, Klassik (light, fresh, no oak) and Reserve (higher alcohol, oak allowed); Burgenland (Hungarian border): sweet wines and reds, dry whites from Pinot Blanc, Welschriesling, Grüner, Chardonnay; Steiermark Sauvignon
P4: Che cosa sono i DAC e come si producono i dolci del Neusiedlersee? || Que sont les DAC et comment produit-on les liquoreux du Neusiedlersee ? || What are DACs, and how are Neusiedlersee sweet wines made?
K: vast majority PDO; PGI Landwein, no GI Wein; four federal states and 16 smaller areas; Qualitätswein from 35 varieties in any style, can carry Prädikat (mainly for medium-sweet and sweet), plus Ausbruch (between BA and TBA) and Strohwein/Schilfwein (bunches dried on straw or reeds in winter); DAC: producers agree variety(ies) and style with regulations; only conforming wines use the name, others only federal state name; nine areas are DACs; Neusiedlersee: vast shallow lake; vineyards on low ground near the lake and wetlands get autumn mists → botrytis almost guaranteed every year; Welschriesling widely used (botrytis-susceptible; dry versions simple, citrus, green apple, high acid)

Q: Tokaji. || Tokaj. || Tokaji.
P6: Spiega come si produce il Tokaji Aszú e perché la regione favorisce la muffa nobile. || Expliquez comment est produit le tokaji aszú et pourquoi la région favorise la pourriture noble. || Explain how Tokaji Aszú is made and why the region favours noble rot.
K: Carpathian foothills, far north-east Hungary; moderate climate; best vineyards on southerly hillside slopes; Bodrog and Tisza rivers and tributaries give early-morning humidity for noble rot; Furmint most planted (late ripening, botrytis-susceptible, high acid, apple → nuts, honey), Hárslevelű (late ripening, perfume), Sárga Muskotály (Muscat, aromatic); pickers separate individual aszú berries (several passes), healthy bunches, and Szamorodni bunches; base wine from healthy grapes; aszú berries macerated before/during/after fermentation for 12–60 hours (traditionally as paste; uncrushed avoids bitterness), pressed, oak-matured; amount of aszú berries sets sweetness (puttony); deep amber, high acidity, orange peel, apricot, honey; 2013 rules: minimum 120 g/L (traditionally 60 g/L, 3–6 puttonyos), at least 18 months oak, release January of third year; lower sugar → Late Harvest or Szamorodni; Aszú Eszencia abolished; sweet in 50 cl bottles
P4: Quali altri stili produce la regione di Tokaj? || Quels autres styles produit la région de Tokaj ? || What other styles does the Tokaj region produce?
K: dry wines without botrytis (mainly Furmint), growing in importance, allow production in low-rot years; simple unoaked to concentrated, new-oak, age-worthy; 75 cl bottles; Szamorodni dry (száraz) or sweet (édes) from partly botrytised bunches; dry aged at least one year in cask, two years old before release, casks not full so flor-like yeast forms (Fino-like); sweet may be oxidative, modern trend full casks; Eszencia: free-run juice of aszú berries, min 450 g/L, ferments for years, under 5% abv, very high acidity, can age a century; modern Late Harvest: grapes left longer on vine, botrytised grapes fermented directly (like Sauternes/TBA), shorter maturation
