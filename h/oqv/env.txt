Q: Ambiente del vigneto: clima e fattori naturali. || Environnement du vignoble : climat et facteurs naturels. || Vineyard environment: climate and natural factors.
P6: Quali sono i bisogni della vite (calore, luce, acqua, nutrienti) e come influiscono latitudine e altitudine? || Quels sont les besoins de la vigne (chaleur, lumière, eau, nutriments) et quel est l'effet de la latitude et de l'altitude ? || What does the vine need (heat, sunlight, water, nutrients), and how do latitude and altitude affect it?
K: five needs: heat, sunlight, water, nutrients, CO2 (always plentiful); below 10°C too cold to grow (dormancy, budburst in spring); amount of growing-season heat decides which varieties grow where (Riesling cool vs Grenache); sunlight drives photosynthesis (glucose for growth and ripening), helps flowering/fruit set; water for photosynthesis and swelling grapes, need rises with temperature (transpiration); mild limitation once canopy grown focuses vine on ripening; nutrients nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium needed only at low levels (excess → vigour, shading; deficiency → chlorosis); latitude: most vineyards 30–50° N and S; further from Equator longer summer days (helps Riesling in Germany); altitude: temperature drops as altitude rises → cool sites near Equator (Cafayate)
P6: Spiega l'effetto di oceani/correnti, montagne, esposizione, nebbia, suolo e venti sul clima del vigneto. || Expliquez l'effet des océans/courants, montagnes, exposition, brouillard, sol et vents sur le climat du vignoble. || Explain the effect of oceans/currents, mountains, aspect, fog, soil and wind on vineyard climate.
K: ocean currents: Humboldt (Chile) and Benguela (South Africa) cool, Gulf Stream warms north-west Europe; seas and lakes warm in winter, cool in summer → low continentality, reduce diurnal range, more cloud, reflected light from rivers/lakes; fog cools (California, Casablanca); dark or stony soils absorb and reradiate heat (vital in cool climates); wet soils warm slowly and delay budburst; aspect: slopes facing the Equator (south in N hemisphere, north in S) get most heat and light, steeper more so (Mosel), middle of slope best; cold air sinks → frost in hollows, mid-slope safer; cold air and fog blowing onshore enhance ocean cooling

Q: Clima, condizioni meteo e annata. || Climat, conditions météo et millésime. || Climate, weather and vintage.
P4: Distingui clima continentale, marittimo e mediterraneo e le classi fresco/moderato/caldo. || Distinguez climats continental, maritime et méditerranéen et les catégories frais/modéré/chaud. || Distinguish continental, maritime and Mediterranean climates and cool/moderate/hot classes.
K: classes by average growing-season temperature (Apr–Oct N, Oct–Apr S): cool ≤16.5°C, moderate 16.5–18.5°C, warm 18.5–21°C, hot >21°C; continental: high continentality, short summers, rapid autumn temperature drop, spring frost risk (cool continental Chablis, Champagne), suit late-budding early-ripening varieties, dry summers → irrigation if hot; maritime: cool to moderate, low continentality, rain spread through year, ripening continues into autumn (Bordeaux ripens Cabernet), but rain can harm flowering, fruit set, health at harvest; Mediterranean: low continentality, warm dry summers (Mediterranean, coastal California, Chile, South Africa, SE Australia) → fuller body, riper tannins, higher alcohol, lower acidity; drought risk; climate = average annual pattern over years, weather = annual variation (Bordeaux 2003 vs 2007; Central Valley predictable)
P6: Quali eventi meteo possono danneggiare un'annata e come rispondono i viticoltori? || Quels événements météo peuvent abîmer un millésime et comment répondent les vignerons ? || What weather events can damage a vintage, and how do growers respond?
K: winter freeze below −20°C damages/kills vine, graft most at risk → earthing up; mild/no winter: no dormancy, shorter vine life, poorer quality, more pests; spring frost (air below 0°C at ground level kills buds/shoots) → heaters (air movement), wind machines (draw warm air from above), sprinklers (freezing water releases heat), site on slopes avoiding hollows, high training; cold spring delays budburst, shortens season; cold or cloud or heavy rain at flowering/fruit set → smaller crop; extreme heat stops vine activity, no ripening; intense sun → sunburn, bitter skins (canopy shade); drought → water stress, wilting, no ripening (irrigation where permitted: drip, sprinklers, flood); too much water → vigour, shading, waterlogging; rain before harvest → swollen diluted or split berries, fungal disease; hail → netting, owning several sites

Q: Suolo e acqua. || Sol et eau. || Soil and water.
P6: Spiega come drenaggio, ritenzione idrica, nutrienti e temperatura del suolo influenzano la qualità con esempi (gesso, ardesia, ghiaia, argilla). || Expliquez comment drainage, rétention d'eau, nutriments et température du sol influencent la qualité, avec des exemples (craie, schiste, graves, argile). || Explain how soil drainage, water retention, nutrients and temperature affect quality, with examples (chalk, slate, gravel, clay).
K: soil = particles (stones, sand largest, clay smallest) and humus over bedrock; ideal: good water early for shoots/leaves, then mild water stress after veraison promotes ripening; water binds to clay and humus — too much clay → waterlogging, kills roots; sand and stones drain, hold little water → irrigation even in rainy areas; loam (sand + clay) drains yet retains enough; no soil always ideal, depends on climate; stones/dark soils reradiate heat (gravel Médoc, slate Mosel, Priorat stones); chalky soils drain and retain water (Champagne chalk, Jerez albariza); nutrients N, P, K needed at low levels, excess → vigour and shading, deficiency → chlorosis (yellow leaves, poor ripening; fertiliser)

Q: Il ciclo annuale della vite e il diurnal range. || Le cycle annuel de la vigne et l'amplitude thermique. || The vine's annual cycle and diurnal range.
P5: Descrivi le fasi del ciclo annuale della vite dal germogliamento alla vendemmia. || Décrivez les étapes du cycle annuel de la vigne du débourrement à la vendange. || Describe the stages of the vine's annual cycle from budburst to harvest.
K: winter dormancy, no green parts (below 10°C); budburst in spring starts growing season, shoots grow; flowering in late spring/early summer and small grapes form (fruit set); summer: grapes grow but hard, green, high acid, no sugar; late summer/early autumn ripening: berries swell with water, sugar rises, acid drops, flavours develop; first sign colour change (véraison) to blue/black, pink or golden; harvest in autumn; leaves drop and vine dormant again; photosynthesis glucose used with nutrients for growth and ripening
P5: Che cosa sono continentalità e escursione termica (diurnal range) e come influenzano lo stile del vino? || Que sont la continentalité et l'amplitude thermique journalière et comment influencent-elles le style ? || What are continentality and diurnal range, and how do they affect wine style?
K: continentality = difference between coldest and hottest months; high inland, low near large bodies of water (heat and cool slowly); determines length of growing season and total heat → which varieties; diurnal range = day vs night temperature; cool nights slow loss of aromas and acidity; warm nights accelerate ripening; in warm/hot climates large diurnal range → fresher, more aromatic wines; seas, lakes, even rivers reduce diurnal range; cloud cover reduces it (clear nights cool fast, cloudy days warm slowly)
