Q: Rioja. || Rioja. || Rioja.
P6: Descrivi le sottoregioni della Rioja e il ruolo di Tempranillo e Garnacha. || Décrivez les sous-régions de la Rioja et le rôle du tempranillo et du grenache. || Describe Rioja's sub-regions and the roles of Tempranillo and Garnacha.
K: three sub-regions around Logroño; Rioja Alavesa: west of Logroño, north bank of Ebro, foothills of Cantabrian Mountains, perhaps lightest wines, often most finesse; Rioja Alta: larger, west of Logroño, mainly south of Ebro; both 500–800 m, moderated by Atlantic, Cantabrian Mountains shield from worst Atlantic weather; Rioja Baja: east of Logroño, mainly south bank, less maritime, hotter summers, more severe winters, low rainfall, drought; plantings dominated by black grapes; Tempranillo most planted, best in cooler western sub-regions, main component: red fruit, medium tannin; benefits from body and alcohol of Garnacha, which grows best in Rioja Baja; Mazuelo and Graciano supporting roles (Graciano: concentrated black fruit, acidity, tannin for ageing; Mazuelo: acidity, tannin, colour); DOCa (one of two with Priorat)
P6: Spiega le categorie d'invecchiamento (Joven, Crianza, Reserva, Gran Reserva) e lo stile tradizionale vs moderno. || Expliquez les catégories de vieillissement (Joven, Crianza, Reserva, Gran Reserva) et le style traditionnel vs moderne. || Explain the ageing categories (Joven, Crianza, Reserva, Gran Reserva) and traditional versus modern style.
K: Spanish law (red minimums): Joven no minimum; Crianza 24 months total, 6 in barrel; Reserva 36 total, 12 in barrel; Gran Reserva 60 total, 18 in barrel; whites/rosés: Reserva 24 total 6 barrel, Gran Reserva 48 total 6 barrel; Rioja requires longer ageing both in total and in barrel; Gran Reserva only exceptional vintages, many producers exceed minimums; early-drinking reds semi-carbonic maceration → vibrant red fruit, low smooth tannin; for long maturation destemmed, crushed, traditional fermentation; some vigorous cap management, extended maceration → deep, extracted, fruity; many now more subtle and elegant; oak defining: traditionally American oak, pronounced vanilla; many now French/European oak for at least some, subtler spice; white Rioja: Viura most planted (eight varieties approved); traditional long American oak ageing, deliberately oxidised, deep golden, nutty — out of favour; modern minimal oxygen contact for fruit; some barrel-fermented, less oxidative

Q: Ribera del Duero, Priorat e la Spagna centrale. || Ribera del Duero, Priorat et l'Espagne centrale. || Ribera del Duero, Priorat and central Spain.
P6: Confronta Ribera del Duero e Priorat (clima, suoli, uve, stili). || Comparez Ribera del Duero et Priorat (climat, sols, cépages, styles). || Compare Ribera del Duero and Priorat (climate, soils, grapes, styles).
K: Ribera del Duero: cut off from maritime influence by ring of mountains; short hot dry summers, very cold winters; highest part of Meseta Central, some vineyards over 850 m → cool nights all year retain acidity and fresh fruit; DO for red and rosé only, mostly red; Tempranillo dominant, only variety in most of the best: dark, high tannin; Cabernet, Malbec, Merlot permitted, rarely used; Garnacha mostly dry rosé; trend: long macerations, relatively short ageing in new oak, increasingly French; Toro similar climate, Tempranillo, full, intense, high alcohol (heat, sun, low-yield bush vines); Priorat: hills inland from Tarragona; long hot dry summers, very low rainfall; old late-ripening Garnacha and Cariñena; llicorella: layers of red slate with mica, reflect and conserve heat, deep, retain water; cool nights; bush vines on steep slopes → mechanisation almost impossible, expensive; low-nutrient soils + old vines → tiny yields → complexity, intensity, never cheap; deep colour, high tannin, medium-high alcohol, concentrated black fruit, often toasty new French oak; some Cabernet added; DOCa (DOQ in Catalan)
P4: Che cosa producono La Mancha, Valdepeñas e i Vinos de Pago? || Que produisent La Mancha, Valdepeñas et les Vinos de Pago ? || What do La Mancha, Valdepeñas and Vinos de Pago produce?
K: Castilla-La Mancha: almost half of Spain's production, Meseta Central south of Madrid, hot continental, freezing winters, very hot dry summers; La Mancha largest DO: Airén most planted (Spain's most planted grape, copes with heat and drought), neutral fresh whites, much for Brandy de Jerez; in decline as authorities encourage Tempranillo (Cencibel) and international varieties (Cabernet, Merlot, Syrah, Chardonnay, Sauvignon); investment in technology → excellent source of inexpensive well-made reds and whites for export; Valdepeñas: just south, identical climate but higher quality reputation; Airén most planted but Tempranillo (Cencibel) main red, varietal or blended with international; fruity to concentrated oak-matured; Vinos de Pago: single estates with high reputation, own grapes only, vinified and matured on estate; trend began in La Mancha, home of most pago estates; Vino de la Tierra = PGI (Castilla y León most extensive)

Q: Spagna del Nord-Ovest e bianchi spagnoli. || Espagne du Nord-Ouest et blancs espagnols. || North-West Spain and Spanish whites.
P6: Descrivi Rías Baixas (Albariño), Rueda (Verdejo) e Bierzo (Mencía). || Décrivez Rías Baixas (albariño), Rueda (verdejo) et Bierzo (mencía). || Describe Rías Baixas (Albariño), Rueda (Verdejo) and Bierzo (Mencía).
K: north-west cooler and wetter (Atlantic) → many of Spain's best whites; Rías Baixas: Atlantic coast, moderate damp climate, mildew and rot common → pergolas for air circulation (some other easier trellises); Albariño thick-skinned resists fungal disease, naturally high acid, ripe citrus/stone fruit; most refreshing unoaked, some richer, touch of oak or lees stirring; red permitted, minority; Rueda: between Toro and Ribera, focus white, continental with cool summer nights; Verdejo highly prone to oxidation (once Sherry-like wines), protective winemaking → light body, high acid, melon, peach, like Sauvignon Blanc (often blended); richer with skin contact and barrel fermentation; varietal Verdejo or Sauvignon, blends at least 50% Verdejo; simple fruity to barrel-fermented; Bierzo: mountains between Galicia and Meseta, moderate, cooling maritime influence; mostly red; Mencía: elegant, naturally high acid, red fruit, sometimes herbaceous; best from old vines on steep stony slopes; unoaked for perfumed fruit or new oak for toast; whites: night/early-morning harvest and cool stainless steel keep delicate fruit
P4: Che cos'è Navarra e perché è nota per i rosati e i tagli internazionali? || Qu'est-ce que la Navarre et pourquoi est-elle connue pour ses rosés et assemblages internationaux ? || What is Navarra, and why is it known for rosé and international blends?
K: from northern/eastern borders of Rioja into Pyrenees foothills; climate like Rioja, cooler and wetter near mountains; Tempranillo most planted, focus red; blended with Rioja varieties (similar style) or increasingly Cabernet and Merlot; good-value Joven to premium Gran Reserva; Garnacha for rosé picked earlier (higher acid, lower sugar), handled protectively → refreshing fruity, medium alcohol; small white output Viura, Chardonnay, Sauvignon; Cariñena and Calatayud (south of Ebro): warm continental, low rain, Garnacha main, fruity cheap or old-vine concentrated; Cariñena grape not the most planted in Cariñena DO

Q: Spagna: clima, vitigni e legislazione. || Espagne : climat, cépages et législation. || Spain: climate, grape varieties and law.
P5: Descrivi le tre zone climatiche spagnole, le sfide viticole e le principali uve nere. || Décrivez les trois zones climatiques espagnoles, les défis viticoles et les principaux cépages noirs. || Describe Spain's three climatic zones, the viticultural challenges and the main black grapes.
K: north/north-west coast moderate maritime, Atlantic, high rainfall; east coast (Catalunya to Levante) warm Mediterranean moderated by sea or altitude, hotter further south; Meseta Central hot continental plateau, cut off by mountains, freezing winters, very hot dry summers, cool nights at altitude; heat and lack of water biggest challenges (except north-west): low fungal disease but vine stress; low-density bush vines maximise water per vine and shade fruit; wire training slowly introduced for machine harvesting; Tempranillo: thick skin, medium acid, best where sea/altitude moderate heat; Joven (semi-carbonic, strawberry) or ageworthy oaked blends; Garnacha high alcohol, matures early, rosados, Priorat old vines; Monastrell thick-skinned, drought tolerant, needs heat (Jumilla, Yecla): very deep, full, high tannin and alcohol, low-medium acid, ripe blackberry; Graciano, Cariñena/Mazuelo, Mencía; best reds oaked with some new oak, American traditionally, now French
P5: Spiega la gerarchia delle denominazioni spagnole (VP, DOCa, DO, VdlT) e il ruolo delle varietà internazionali e della Catalogna. || Expliquez la hiérarchie des appellations espagnoles (VP, DOCa, DO, VdlT) et le rôle des cépages internationaux et de la Catalogne. || Explain Spain's appellation hierarchy (VP, DOCa, DO, VdlT) and the role of international varieties and Catalunya.
K: PDO = DOP but traditional terms: Vinos de Pago (single estates, high reputation, own grapes vinified/matured on estate), DOCa (DOs with at least ten years' standing may apply; only Rioja and Priorat, DOQ in Catalan), DO (minimum quality, rules on varieties, viticulture, location); PGI Vino de la Tierra; outside = Vino; age categories Joven, Crianza, Reserva, Gran Reserva; international varieties (Cabernet, Merlot, Sauvignon, Chardonnay) widespread esp. north-east where local varieties showed limited potential, sometimes blended with local; Catalunya: generic region-wide DO to allow blending for volumes of branded wine; nearly all Cava (Parellada, Xarel-lo, Macabeo); Penedès three climatic zones (hot coastal plain, warm valleys, moderate hills up to 800 m) → wide range, Cava grapes plus Chardonnay, Sauvignon, Gewurztraminer, Merlot, Cabernet, Tempranillo (Ull de Llebre), Pinot Noir; Levante: Valencia value, Monastrell, Merseguera, Moscatel de Valencia (sweet fortified Muscat of Alexandria); Jumilla, Yecla hot arid Monastrell youthful fruity
