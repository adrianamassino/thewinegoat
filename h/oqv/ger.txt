Q: Germania: clima e Riesling della Mosella. || Allemagne : climat et riesling de Moselle. || Germany: climate and Mosel Riesling.
P6: Spiega perché i migliori vigneti della Mosella sono su pendii ripidi vicino al fiume e che ruolo hanno i suoli di ardesia. || Expliquez pourquoi les meilleures vignes de Moselle sont sur des pentes raides près de la rivière et le rôle des sols de schiste. || Explain why the best Mosel vineyards are on steep slopes near the river and the role of slate soils.
K: cool continental, marginal; summers can be wet, rain declines in autumn; long cool ripening gives sugar while keeping acidity; vintage variation; best sites steep, stony, southerly aspect maximise heat and sunlight; slate on surface gives extra warmth; river reflects sunlight and moving air protects from frost; worked by hand, winches; vines head-pruned, individually staked, canes tied at top for light and airflow; Middle Mosel (Piesport, Bernkastel, Wehlen) best sites steep slate slopes next to river; Saar and Ruwer cooler, tiny share, even higher acidity, unripe vintages go to sparkling
P4: Descrivi lo stile tipico di un Riesling della Mosella e come cambia con l'invecchiamento. || Décrivez le style typique d'un riesling de Moselle et son évolution au vieillissement. || Describe the typical style of a Mosel Riesling and how it changes with age.
K: lighter body, lower alcohol, higher acidity than Rheingau, Rheinhessen, Pfalz; floral and green fruit; dry to lusciously sweet; Kabinett light, high acid, green apple/citrus, sweet styles 8–9% abv (best by stopping fermentation, not Süssreserve), drier up to 12%; Spätlese riper, citrus and stone fruit; Riesling is the only permitted variety for Mosel GG; Riesling very long-lived, stays fresh, develops honey, toast, sometimes petrol while retaining high acidity

Q: Leggi tedesche sul vino e Prädikate. || Lois allemandes et Prädikate. || German wine law and Prädikate.
P6: Elenca i Prädikate dal Kabinett al Trockenbeerenauslese e spiega su quale criterio si basano e quali stili producono. || Citez les Prädikate du Kabinett au Trockenbeerenauslese, le critère sur lequel ils reposent et les styles obtenus. || List the Prädikate from Kabinett to Trockenbeerenauslese, explain their basis and the styles they produce.
K: classified by minimum must weight at harvest; harvest spread over weeks/months with several passes; Prädikate indicate style; Kabinett (lowest, dry to medium, most delicate), Spätlese (riper, more concentrated, dry to medium), Auslese (individually selected extra-ripe bunches, dry to sweet, noble rot often), Beerenauslese (sweet only, noble rot typical but not essential), Eiswein (sweet only, not noble rot, varietal purity, fine acid-sweetness balance, rare; careful yeast, no MLF or new oak), Trockenbeerenauslese (sweet only, noble rot essential); BA/TBA sweet, low alcohol, honey, dried stone fruit, candied peel, flowers, not every year; Prädikatswein mostly white; nearly all have residual sugar but up to Auslese can be dry
P4: Che cosa indicano Qualitätswein, trocken/halbtrocken e la classificazione VDP (Grosse Lage, Grosses Gewächs)? || Qu'indiquent Qualitätswein, trocken/halbtrocken et le classement VDP (Grosse Lage, Grosses Gewächs) ? || What do Qualitätswein, trocken/halbtrocken and the VDP classification (Grosse Lage, Grosses Gewächs) indicate?
K: vast majority PDO; 13 regions not linked to varieties; varietal labelling common; PDO from one region named on label; Qualitätswein lowest must weight, dry to medium, many dry wines of all levels sold as Qualitätswein (trend to dry for domestic market); PGI = Landwein, no GI = Deutscher Wein; trocken = dry, halbtrocken = off-dry/medium (legal), feinherb not legally defined; sweetness often deduced from category or alcohol; village + vineyard (Piesporter Goldtröpfchen), but groups of vineyards labelled the same way (Piesporter Michelsberg), price best guide; no legal Premier/Grand Cru; VDP independent producers' group classified their vineyards; GG = dry Qualitätswein from best sites, embossed GG and grapes on bottle neck; VDP trademark not law; permitted varieties vary by region

Q: Regioni tedesche oltre la Mosella. || Régions allemandes hors Moselle. || German regions beyond the Mosel.
P5: Confronta Rheingau, Nahe, Pfalz e Baden. || Comparez Rheingau, Nahe, Pfalz et Baden. || Compare Rheingau, Nahe, Pfalz and Baden.
K: Nahe: between Mosel and Rheinhessen; best on steep south-facing slopes between Schlossböckelheim and Bad Kreuznach; Riesling most planted, style between Mosel and fuller regions, Mosel acidity with riper fruit; Rheingau: small, prestigious, north bank of Rhine and Main, southerly aspect, Taunus hills shelter; Johannisberg, Rüdesheim; mostly dry Riesling, medium-full, ripe peach; Rhine humidity → great BA/TBA; Hochheim (Main) warmer, fuller; Spätburgunder at Assmannshausen; Rheinhessen largest, many varieties, Müller-Thurgau and Riesling most planted, ~30% black (Dornfelder, Portugieser, Spätburgunder), Rheinterrasse/Nierstein fullest Rieslings, innovation (Worms); Pfalz second largest, 80 km strip continuing Alsace, east of Haardt (Vosges) → driest region, water stress; Riesling most planted, Dornfelder second, almost 40% black, Mittelhaardt (Forst, Deidesheim) ripe full Riesling, mostly drier styles; Baden warmest, most southerly, fullest, highest alcohol, third largest, Kaiserstuhl (extinct volcano) and Tuniberg opposite Colmar, Spätburgunder most planted; Franken Silvaner
P5: Che cosa si sa di Spätburgunder, Müller-Thurgau e Silvaner in Germania? || Que sait-on du spätburgunder, du müller-thurgau et du silvaner en Allemagne ? || What should you know about Spätburgunder, Müller-Thurgau and Silvaner in Germany?
K: Spätburgunder (Pinot Noir) third most planted variety, warmer areas, especially Pfalz and Baden, dry, light fruity or concentrated with oak, growing reputation; Dornfelder deep coloured; Portugieser, Trollinger, Schwarzriesling (Meunier) light fruity, domestic; Müller-Thurgau (Rivaner) = Riesling × Madeleine Royale (1880s), earlier ripening, less acid and intensity, floral fruity, rarely high quality, second most planted white, declining with market for inexpensive medium wines; Silvaner third white, Rheinhessen and Franken, dry and sweet, less acid and fruit than Riesling, earthy; in Franken early flowering/ripening and frost-prone so planted on warmest sites → concentration, dry, rich, earthy, flask-shaped bottles (Würzburg); Grauburgunder and Weissburgunder growing, usually dry
