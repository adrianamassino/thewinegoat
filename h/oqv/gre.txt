Q: Grecia. || Grèce. || Greece.
P6: Descrivi l'Assyrtiko di Santorini, compresa la coltivazione a «kouloura», e lo Xinomavro di Naoussa. || Décrivez l'assyrtiko de Santorin, avec la conduite en « kouloura », et le xinomavro de Naoussa. || Describe Santorini Assyrtiko, including kouloura training, and Naoussa Xinomavro.
K: Santorini: windswept volcanic Aegean island; winds so strong a unique training is used: permanent wood trained very low into a basket shape, fruit grows inside, protecting vines and grapes; PDO covers red and white, dry and sweet; Assyrtiko whites highest reputation: perfumed, concentrated ripe citrus and stone fruit, natural high acidity; Vinsanto: late-harvested grapes sun-dried up to 14 days, at least two years in old oak (often longer), luscious sweetness balanced by high acidity, older ones oxidative caramel and nuts; Naoussa PDO: Macedonia, northern Greece, vineyards up to 400 m, cooler; exclusively Xinomavro, compared with Nebbiolo: high tannin and acidity, medium colour fading quickly to tawny, little fresh fruit even young, long-lived, spice and earth; some producers now deeper coloured, less tannic or new oak
P4: Che cosa caratterizza il Nemea Agiorgitiko e qual è il ruolo di clima e vitigni autoctoni in Grecia? || Qu'est-ce qui caractérise le Nemea agiorgitiko et quel est le rôle du climat et des cépages autochtones en Grèce ? || What characterises Nemea Agiorgitiko, and what is the role of climate and native grapes in Greece?
K: hot Mediterranean, summers over 30°C; sea, altitude and strong winds (south-east islands) cool; best vineyards in cooler sites; little growing-season rain → water stress even in wetter west; drought especially in eastern rain shadow with little irrigation water; over 200 native varieties, adapted to hot arid conditions, unique selling point; most PDOs require native varieties; Nemea PDO (north Peloponnese) exclusively Agiorgitiko, 230–900 m; best fruit from middle altitudes; lower hotter slopes jammy, early-drinking fruity; higher slopes higher acid, less fine tannins, for rosé or freshness in blends; best deep ruby, high smooth tannins, low-moderate acidity, sweet spice, red fruit, affinity for new oak, age well; labels PDO, Appellation d'origine de qualité supérieure, Prostatevomeni Onomasia Proelefsis
