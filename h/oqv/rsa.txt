P6: Quali influenze climatiche e varietà caratterizzano Stellenbosch, Constantia, Walker Bay e Swartland? || Quelles influences climatiques et quels cépages caractérisent Stellenbosch, Constantia, Walker Bay et Swartland ? || What climatic influences and grapes characterise Stellenbosch, Constantia, Walker Bay and Swartland?
K: Stellenbosch: from near coast at Somerset West into mountains; long hub of fine wine; moderate to warm, sufficient winter rain, summers rarely too hot thanks to cool False Bay winds funnelled by mountains into valleys; above all variety of altitude, aspect, soil in small area, divided into wards; top reds: Cabernet and Merlot (Bordeaux blends), Syrah, Cape red blends; cooler sites Sauvignon and Chardonnay; Constantia ward (Cape Peninsula): oldest vineyards, eastern flanks of Table Mountain, constantly cooled by Cape Doctor, reputation for Sauvignon Blanc; one estate makes sweet Vin de Constance from late-harvested Muscat; Cape Point Sauvignon and Semillon; Walker Bay: well exposed to ocean, Hemel-en-Aarde wards, long home of some of the best Chardonnay and Pinot Noir; Swartland: north of Cape Town, once inexpensive bulk, now innovation and premium: old-vine Chenin Blanc, high-quality Syrah, often dry-farmed (lower yields, more concentration), premium red and white Cape blends; Darling: coastal breezes, Sauvignon, old vines
P4: Descrivi Chenin Blanc e Pinotage in Sudafrica. || Décrivez chenin blanc et pinotage en Afrique du Sud. || Describe Chenin Blanc and Pinotage in South Africa.
K: Chenin most planted variety of either colour; importance declined but versatility exploited: great sweet and dry; basic easy-drinking simple stone fruit; old bush vines → more concentrated, complex, fuller texture; barrel fermentation and ageing add body and toast; Swartland white blends Chenin + Rhône varieties (Marsanne, Roussanne, Grenache Blanc, Viognier); Pinotage = Pinot Noir × Cinsault, developed in South Africa, hardly grown elsewhere → point of difference; light fruity red berry style or full-bodied rich spiced berry from old bush vines; with heavily toasted oak staves → intense coffee or chocolate aromas, marketed as unique style, popular; blended with international varieties → Cape blend

Q: Sudafrica: legislazione e regioni. || Afrique du Sud : législation et régions. || South Africa: wine law and regions.
P5: Come funziona il sistema Wine of Origin e perché la GI Western Cape è importante? || Comment fonctionne le système Wine of Origin et pourquoi l'IG Western Cape est-elle importante ? || How does the Wine of Origin system work, and why is the Western Cape GI important?
K: Wine of Origin (W.O.) scheme, Certification Seal on every bottle; four sizes of unit: geographical units (e.g. Western Cape, widely used), regions (e.g. Coastal Region, Breede River Valley), districts (Stellenbosch, Paarl, Swartland, Walker Bay, Elgin, Worcester, Robertson), wards (Constantia, Durbanville, Elim, Hemel-en-Aarde); estate wine: single estate, estate vineyards in one area, vinified and bottled on estate; Integrated Production of Wine (IPW) sustainability certification; well over 90% from Western Cape; some wineries source beyond their district/ward and label as region or Western Cape — several premium wines made this way (blending across areas); Orange River (Northern Cape) very hot, irrigated, inexpensive whites; Worcester (Breede River west): hot dry, irrigation from river, fertile, large volume Chenin and Colombard, much distilled, reliable brand wines; Robertson slightly cooler (south-easterly winds), less fertile outcrops → excellent Syrah and full Chardonnay; Paarl hotter inland, cool nights, Cabernet, Syrah, Pinotage, Chenin, Chardonnay; Elgin altitude, intense fresh Sauvignon; Elim pungent herbaceous Sauvignon; Durbanville sea breezes Sauvignon
P5: Perché il clima del Capo è più fresco di quanto la latitudine suggerirebbe? || Pourquoi le climat du Cap est-il plus frais que ne le suggère la latitude ? || Why is the Cape's climate cooler than its latitude suggests?
K: 27–34°S should be hot; inland summers can exceed 30°C; most important cooling from Southern Ocean: cold Benguela Current from Antarctic up the west coast cools coastal areas; magnified by strong south-easterly summer winds (Cape Doctor) bringing ocean-cooled air inland, lowering temperatures a further couple of degrees; mountain ranges give range of altitudes, aspects and soils: valley floors much warmer than mountains; southern (cooler) slopes in Southern Hemisphere; mountain shadows cool parts of the day; mountains channel winds near coast; varieties: Cabernet most planted black (varietal, Bordeaux blends), Merlot plummy, Syrah rich in heat/peppery in cool, Pinot Noir only coolest coastal areas; Sauvignon best in coolest (pure citrus, green fruit), early-picked in warm; Chardonnay exceptional in cooler sites, Burgundian techniques; Hanepoot (Muscat of Alexandria) late harvest; premium reds oaked with some new
