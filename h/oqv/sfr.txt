Q: Sud della Francia: Languedoc, Roussillon e Provenza. || Sud de la France : Languedoc, Roussillon et Provence. || Southern France: Languedoc, Roussillon and Provence.
P5: Che cosa offre la denominazione IGP Pays d'Oc e perché è commercialmente importante? || Qu'offre l'IGP Pays d'Oc et pourquoi est-elle commercialement importante ? || What does IGP Pays d'Oc offer, and why is it commercially important?
K: more IGP wine made here than anywhere else in France, vital for commercial survival of grape growing; Pays d'Oc covers all départements of Languedoc-Roussillon; wider range of permitted varieties (international: Merlot, Cabernet, Chardonnay, Sauvignon, Viognier) and varietal labelling; warm sunny climate gives large volumes of ripe fruity wine easily → excellent value; vineyard composition changed dramatically in last 20 years (local + international)
P5: Quali sono le caratteristiche delle AC rosse del Languedoc (es. Corbières, Minervois, Faugères) e del rosato di Provenza? || Quelles sont les caractéristiques des AC rouges du Languedoc (Corbières, Minervois, Faugères) et du rosé de Provence ? || What characterises Languedoc red ACs (e.g. Corbières, Minervois, Faugères) and Provence rosé?
K: Languedoc generic AC from Spanish border to Nîmes; best sub-regions add name (Pic-St-Loup); more specific ACs (Côtes du Roussillon, Minervois) with own rules and sub-appellations (Minervois-La Livinière; Corbières 11 sub-regions, Boutenac own AC); mostly red from Carignan, Grenache, Syrah, less Mourvèdre; cooler areas more finesse, lighter tannins, best Syrah; warmer areas Mourvèdre, more body, meaty gamey; best full, concentrated spicy red fruit, often oak; rosés soft fruity; Côtes du Roussillon rugged, sunny, dry, windy → concentrated (Villages in north); Fitou warm coastal strip fuller, cooler inland altitude lighter; Corbières warm coast and altitude cooled by tramontane; Minervois slopes of Massif Central, Atlantic cooling; Provence: vast majority rosé, very pale, light, dry, delicate grapefruit and red fruit; Côtes de Provence largest; hills protect from mistral; Bandol Mourvèdre reds on south-facing terraces, dark, full, powerful tannins, need bottle age (bramble, meat, liquorice)

Q: Clima e varietà del Sud della Francia. || Climat et cépages du Sud de la France. || Southern France climate and grape varieties.
P6: Descrivi il clima del Sud della Francia, i venti e il ruolo di Grenache, Syrah, Carignan, Mourvèdre e Cinsault. || Décrivez le climat du Sud de la France, les vents et le rôle de grenache, syrah, carignan, mourvèdre et cinsault. || Describe Southern France's climate and winds, and the roles of Grenache, Syrah, Carignan, Mourvèdre and Cinsault.
K: warm Mediterranean, summers often over 30°C, mild winters, low rainfall especially in growing season; drought biggest challenge, fungal disease less than anywhere in France, occasional summer floods; inland foothills cooler, less fertile well-drained soils better for quality than coastal plain; cool dry northern winds: mistral down Rhône valley, tramontane through gap between Massif Central and Pyrenees; Grenache suits warm dry summer; Syrah better on cooler sites; Carignan high tannin, acidity, colour but can lack fruit/finesse, once planted for huge yields, now reduced, still permitted in all ACs, old vines on poor soils can be quality; semi-carbonic maceration softens its tannins; Cinsault for rosé or red fruit in blends; Mourvèdre only in warmest sites, adds richness, colour, complexity; modern equipment, hygiene, temperature control, oxygen management, judicious oak
P4: Quali uve bianche e stili si trovano nel Sud della Francia? || Quels cépages blancs et styles trouve-t-on dans le Sud de la France ? || Which white grapes and styles are found in Southern France?
K: non-traditional varieties now dominate: Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc most planted (IGP), significant Viognier; local Muscat (sweet fortified and some dry grapey) and Grenache Blanc (dry, soft peachy, full, low acid, oxidises easily, blended); local specialities: Picpoul in Pinet (refreshing high acidity, green fruit, citrus; coastal sea breezes keep acidity), Mauzac in Limoux, Maccabeu in Roussillon, Rolle in Provence, Clairette in Languedoc and Provence; Limoux altitude → premium oaked Chardonnay; best Provence whites feature aromatic Rolle or Clairette
