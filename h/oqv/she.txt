Q: Sherry: invecchiamento biologico. || Xérès : élevage biologique. || Sherry: biological ageing.
P6: Spiega la produzione di un Fino dalla vendemmia all'imbottigliamento, compresi flor e solera. || Expliquez la production d'un fino de la vendange à la mise en bouteille, flor et solera compris. || Explain how a Fino is made from harvest to bottling, including flor and solera.
K: Palomino (low acid, neutral); grapes to press quickly to avoid oxidation (heat); fermentation in large steel tanks at 20–25°C (high for white) → neutral dry base wine; first classification in autumn: lighter, paler wines with finesse for biological ageing (cooler coastal vineyards and/or lower fermentation temperatures more likely); flor already growing on all wines; fortified with 96% neutral spirit to 15–15.5% (ideal for flor); sobretabla few months, critical: see if flor develops; second classification, failures refortified for oxidative or rejected; solera system, 600 L butts (seasoned, no oak flavour) five-sixths full for oxygen access; flor: several yeast strains, thick layer, feeds on alcohol (and nutrients) and oxygen → CO2 and acetaldehyde (unique flavour); protects wine from oxidation; cannot thrive above 15.5%, needs cool-moderate temperatures and high humidity, most vigorous spring/autumn; bodegas thick whitewashed walls, high ceilings, windows to poniente, damp earth floors; frequent regular withdrawals feed nutrients; average age rarely over 3–4 years (flor fails around 7); Fino/Manzanilla pale lemon, citrus, almond, herbs, bready, tangy/salty; do not improve in bottle, drink fresh; en rama minimal fining/filtration; Manzanilla de Sanlúcar separate DO, cooler humid coast → thick flor all year → more intensely tangy
P4: Perché la solera dà un vino costante? || Pourquoi la solera donne-t-elle un vin constant ? || Why does the solera system give a consistent wine?
K: groups of butts (levels) of different average ages: criaderas and solera (oldest level); wine for bottling taken from the solera, equal amount from each butt, never fully emptied; refilled with equal volumes from each butt of first criadera, mixed together, then used to top up each solera butt; first criadera refilled from second, etc.; youngest criadera usually from sobretabla wine; three to many criaderas; only average age can be stated; wine introduced takes on characteristics of the older wine → same wine every time; consistency lost if poor wine fed in or too much drawn; each level fixed to one system; criaderas often in different buildings to limit catastrophic loss; for oxidative systems young wine preserves base character from over-oxidation; most Sherries blends of several solera systems

Q: Sherry ossidativi e dolci. || Xérès oxydatifs et doux. || Oxidative and sweet Sherries.
P6: Confronta Oloroso, Amontillado e Palo Cortado in produzione e stile. || Comparez oloroso, amontillado et palo cortado en production et en style. || Compare Oloroso, Amontillado and Palo Cortado in production and style.
K: Oloroso: darker, richer, heavier wines at first classification (warmer inland vineyards and/or higher fermentation temperatures more likely); fortified to 17% → flor dies; sobretabla less critical; only oxidative ageing, oxygen in part-filled butts; brown, full-bodied, toffee, leather, spice, walnut; very old ones concentrated, astringent, balanced by blending with younger; can age 30+ years, alcohol rises to 22% as water evaporates; Amontillado: biological ageing then refortified to 17% to kill flor and fed into Amontillado solera → oxidative; amber/brown, less full than Oloroso, yeast-derived plus oxidative aromas, yeast notes fade but stay recognisable; Palo Cortado: rare, aroma of Amontillado with body and richness of Oloroso; some identify at second classification; various methods; hard to tell apart; nearly always very high quality; Amontillado, Oloroso, Palo Cortado must now be dry (Oloroso dulce no longer allowed)
P4: Come si producono gli Sherry dolci naturali (PX) e quelli dolcificati (Pale Cream, Medium, Cream)? || Comment produit-on les Xérès doux naturels (PX) et les Xérès adoucis (Pale Cream, Medium, Cream) ? || How are naturally sweet Sherries (PX) and sweetened Sherries (Pale Cream, Medium, Cream) made?
K: PX: thin skin, suited to sun-drying; little planted in Jerez, most grapes from Montilla-Moriles (unusual rule permits); Muscat of Alexandria small amounts; grapes sun-dried to concentrate sugar, develop raisin; pressed, yeast struggle, rarely more than a few degrees; fortified; oxidative ageing; PX deep brown, lusciously sweet, often 500 g/L, dried fruit, coffee, liquorice; Muscat similar plus dried citrus peel; naturally sweet wines rare, often used for blending; Pale Cream: short biological ageing, sweetened typically with RCGM, Fino-like appearance, rarely pronounced flor; Medium: both biological and oxidative characters; Cream: only oxidative; premium sweetened with PX; best balance toffee, leather, walnut with dried fruit; cheap ones cloying, lack complexity

Q: Sherry: vigneto e indicazioni d'età. || Xérès : vignoble et indications d'âge. || Sherry: vineyard and age indications.
P5: Descrivi clima, venti e suolo albariza della zona di Jerez e le uve permesse. || Décrivez climat, vents et sol d'albariza de la zone de Jerez et les cépages autorisés. || Describe the climate, winds and albariza soil of the Jerez area, and the permitted grapes.
K: around Jerez de la Frontera; wines must be matured in Jerez, Sanlúcar de Barrameda or El Puerto de Santa María; hot sunny Mediterranean; coastal vineyards cooler, benefit from cool humid westerly poniente; hot drying easterly levante stresses vines, damages grapes; annual rain high for Spain but little in growing season; albariza: very high chalk, good drainage, depth and excellent water-holding sustain vines through summer; pits dug between rows in autumn/winter to trap water and reduce run-off, smoothed in spring; summer hard crust limits evaporation; Palomino vast majority (naturally low acid, no obvious varietal aromas → ideal, style from ageing); PX little varietal flavour, thin skin for sun-drying, mostly from Montilla-Moriles; Muscat of Alexandria small amounts, sweet wines
P3: Che cosa indicano VORS, VOS, 12 e 15 anni? || Qu'indiquent VORS, VOS, 12 et 15 ans ? || What do VORS, VOS, 12 and 15 years old indicate?
K: four age-indicated categories; VORS (Very Old Rare Sherry) average age at least 30 years; VOS (Very Old Sherry) at least 20 years; each batch tested; 12 and 15 years old apply to a whole solera system, used more flexibly; only Amontillado, Palo Cortado, Oloroso and PX qualify
