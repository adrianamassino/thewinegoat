Q: Spumanti: metodi di produzione. || Effervescents : méthodes de production. || Sparkling wines: production methods.
P6: Descrivi le fasi del metodo tradizionale dal vino base alla tappatura. || Décrivez les étapes de la méthode traditionnelle du vin de base au bouchage. || Describe the stages of the traditional method from base wine to corking.
K: best grapes hand-picked whole unsplit bunches; pressed ASAP, gently, whole bunches, no crushing → minimal tannin and colour (vital for black grapes); EU limits pressure and extraction; base wine: first fermentation usually temperature-controlled steel (some oak), completely dry, neutral, high acid, 10–11% abv; optional MLF and/or oak; reserve wines stored for future; blending: house style despite vintage variation, balance (Chardonnay citrus, finesse, longevity; Pinot Noir red fruit, body), complexity (old reserve dried fruit, oak-matured wines broader texture, spice); many small parcels; liqueur de tirage (wine, sugar, yeast, nutrients, clarifying agent), crown cap with plastic cup; bottles horizontal, cool constant temperature, slow fermentation, +1.2–1.3% abv, CO2 dissolves, 5–6 atmospheres; yeast autolysis: dead yeast break down → bread, biscuit, toast; lasts 4–5 years (up to 10); longer → more yeast character; lees keep wine fresh; riddling: pupitre by hand up to eight weeks, now gyropalette (500 bottles, faster, bulk); disgorgement: neck frozen in cold brine, crown cap removed, pressure ejects frozen plug; topped up with liqueur d'expédition (wine + sugar = dosage → sweetness, balances acidity, flavour development; a second blending); cork + wire cage; seconds → no pressure loss, less oxidation; post-disgorgement ageing few months to integrate dosage; disgorgement dates on some labels
P4: Confronta metodo tradizionale e metodo in autoclave (tank) nei risultati e nei costi. || Comparez méthode traditionnelle et cuve close en résultats et en coûts. || Compare the traditional method and tank method in results and costs.
K: traditional: second fermentation in the bottle in which sold; costly, time-consuming; premium wines commanding highest prices; bread/toast autolytic flavours; labelled 'traditional method'/méthode traditionnelle; transfer method: same up to riddling, then bottles emptied into pressurised tank, filtered, liqueur d'expédition, rebottled → good quality cheaper, consistency in large batches, often labelled 'bottle-fermented'; tank method: first fermentation in steel to keep pure fruit and floral, usually no MLF/oak; yeast, sugar, nutrients, clarifiers added, second fermentation in sealed pressure tank; filtered, bottled under pressure; usually no autolysis (short lees time), paddles can stir lees for some; retains base wine flavours → ideal for aromatic Muscat, Riesling and fruity Prosecco; cheaper, faster, less labour; once seen inferior but high-quality grapes and care → high quality

Q: Champagne. || Champagne. || Champagne.
P6: Spiega come il clima della Champagne e le tre uve principali determinano lo stile del vino. || Expliquez comment le climat champenois et les trois cépages principaux déterminent le style du vin. || Explain how Champagne's climate and its three main grapes determine its style.
K: cool continental: winter freeze, spring frosts, rainy cloudy weather; even warmest years low sugar, very high acidity → ideal for sparkling; frost biggest challenge → most vineyards on slopes; chalk soils drain after storms, retain water in dry periods; sustainability targets; one appellation Champagne AC; 44 premier cru and 17 grand cru villages (whole village rated, no stricter yields), not separate appellations; five sub-regions: Montagne de Reims, Vallée de la Marne, Côte des Blancs (near Reims, Épernay), Côte de Sézanne, Côte des Bar; Chardonnay (Côte des Blancs, Sézanne): much lighter than Burgundy, high acid, floral, citrus; Pinot Noir (Montagne de Reims, Côte des Bar): more body, structural backbone, red fruit; Meunier (Vallée de la Marne): buds late avoiding frost, fruity, for wines drunk young; hand-picked whole bunches, cuvée (purest juice) vs taille; best only cuvée; steel or oak, MLF optional; blending crucial given vintage variation, dozens of wines for house style; NV lighter, fresher, reserve wines complexity; vintage only best years, more concentrated, toasty, biscuity; rosé, Blanc de Blancs (light-medium, citrus), Blanc de Noirs (fuller, red fruit), Prestige Cuvées closed young
P4: Indica le regole di invecchiamento sui lieviti per Champagne NV, millesimato, Crémant e Cava, e i termini di dolcezza Brut Nature, Brut e Demi-Sec. || Indiquez les règles d'élevage sur lies pour Champagne NM, millésimé, crémant et cava, et les termes Brut Nature, Brut et Demi-Sec. || Give the lees-ageing rules for NV and vintage Champagne, Crémant and Cava, and the sweetness terms Brut Nature, Brut and Demi-Sec.
K: Champagne NV min 15 months total incl. at least 12 on lees; vintage min 36 months (no extra lees requirement); in practice often longer; Crémant min 9 months on lees; Cava min 9 months on lees; Saumur and Vouvray min 9 months; Cap Classique 9 months (association recommends 12); EU terms (range per term, used at lower/upper end → house style): Brut Nature/zero dosage 0–3 g/L, no added sugar; Brut 0–12 g/L (by far most popular Champagne style); Demi-Sec 32–50 g/L; Extra Brut, Extra Dry, Sec, Doux also; copied worldwide but compulsory only for EU sale

Q: Spumanti del mondo. || Effervescents du monde. || Sparkling wines of the world.
P6: Confronta Cava, Prosecco e Asti (zone, uve, metodo, stile). || Comparez cava, prosecco et asti (zones, cépages, méthode, style). || Compare Cava, Prosecco and Asti (areas, grapes, method, style).
K: Cava: unusual DO, non-contiguous areas across Spain, vast majority Catalan vineyards around Sant Sadurní d'Anoia (also Navarra, Rioja, Valencia); traditional method, min 9 months lees; Macabeo (Viura), Xarel-lo, Parellada for whites, Garnacha and Monastrell for rosés; Chardonnay and Pinot Noir added more recently (extra fruit and acidity, controversial vs point of difference); mostly dry, medium acidity, autolytic character different from Champagne's bread/toast; lower acidity than most Champagne, approachable; mostly NV, ready on release; some premium long-aged; Prosecco: north-east Italy, Prosecco DOC (Veneto, Friuli) and higher-quality Conegliano-Valdobbiadene DOCG (steep limestone hills, Cartizze and Rive exceptional sites); grape Glera; tank method, medium acid, green apple, melon; Brut, Extra-Dry, Dry with more residual sugar than most Champagne/Cava (fermentation interrupted by chilling and filtering); drink young; Asti DOCG: Piemonte, Muscat Blanc à Petits Grains, Asti method: one fermentation; juice chilled and stored, warmed when needed, CO2 escapes then tank sealed; stopped by chilling at ~7% abv, 5–6 atm, filtered under pressure; sweet, low alcohol, peach, grape, floral, no autolysis, drink young
P4: Come ottengono eleganza gli spumanti metodo tradizionale di California, Australia/Tasmania, Nuova Zelanda e Sudafrica? || Comment les effervescents de méthode traditionnelle de Californie, Australie/Tasmanie, Nouvelle-Zélande et Afrique du Sud obtiennent-ils de l'élégance ? || How do traditional-method sparkling wines from California, Australia/Tasmania, New Zealand and South Africa achieve elegance?
K: sufficiently cool climate fundamental: sugar/acid change slowly alongside flavour; warm regions must pick early → green herbaceous; California: vast majority of US sparkling, best from cooler Los Carneros and Anderson Valley, traditional method, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, extended lees ageing (five years not uncommon), concentrated complex; cheap tank/carbonation from Central Valley, fruity, medium-sweet; Australia: Yarra Valley, Adelaide Hills, Tasmania complex elegant Chardonnay/Pinot, many styles; inexpensive from Riverina by tank/carbonation; NZ: southerly, maritime, cool sea breezes → slow ripening of Chardonnay and Pinot; all regions except Auckland, Marlborough largest volume; North Island richer; sparkling Sauvignon by carbonation/tank; South Africa: Méthode Cap Classique, Western Cape fruit, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir; warm climate → site selection: hillside slopes facing south-east or near coast; association recommends 12 months lees, minimum 9

Q: Spumanti: viticoltura, altri metodi e stili. || Effervescents : viticulture, autres méthodes et styles. || Sparkling wines: viticulture, other methods and styles.
P5: Quali requisiti deve avere l'uva per spumante e perché i climi freschi sono fondamentali? || Quelles exigences doit remplir le raisin pour effervescent et pourquoi les climats frais sont-ils fondamentaux ? || What must grapes for sparkling wine be like, and why are cool climates fundamental?
K: relatively low sugar (second fermentation adds 1.2–1.3% abv, aim for dry base 10–11% abv); high acidity for refreshing style; yet flavours ripe with no green herbaceous character; cool regions: sugar and acid change slowly and together with flavour development; warm regions: sugar rises and acid falls quickly → must pick early, often still green; cool climate fundamental for premium; hand-picking whole unsplit bunches for best; machine harvesting in warm regions for cheap wines to pick before sugar rises/acid falls; gentle prompt pressing, whole bunches
P5: Descrivi il metodo per carbonazione e gli stili Non-vintage, Vintage, Rosé, Blanc de Blancs, Blanc de Noirs, Crémant e Sekt. || Décrivez la gazéification et les styles non millésimé, millésimé, rosé, blanc de blancs, blanc de noirs, crémant et sekt. || Describe carbonation and the Non-vintage, Vintage, Rosé, Blanc de Blancs, Blanc de Noirs, Crémant and Sekt styles.
K: carbonation: CO2 injected into still wine, bottled under pressure; retains base flavours (e.g. Sauvignon Blanc); cheapest; NV: grapes from more than one vintage, standard offering, house style; Vintage: in Champagne single year, other regions may allow small % other years; only best years in variable regions → premium; rosé by blending red and white base wines or short maceration, colour adjusted with liqueur d'expédition; Blanc de Blancs only white grapes; Blanc de Noirs white wine only from black grapes; Prestige Cuvée not a legal term, best wine; Crémant: French ACs (d'Alsace, de Bourgogne, de Loire), traditional method, min 9 months lees, regional still-wine grapes (Alsace no Muscat/Gewurztraminer, Chardonnay allowed); Loire second sparkling centre: Saumur (Chenin, Chardonnay, Cabernet Franc; also red), Vouvray (mostly Chenin; high acid, less bready, smoky/toasty autolysis); Germany highest per capita consumption: Sekt mostly tank method, base wines often from France/Italy; Deutscher Sekt only German grapes, best Riesling; Deutscher Sekt b.A. from one quality region; top estates traditional method single vineyards; Australian sparkling red (mostly Shiraz): full, red berry, smooth tannins, some residual sugar
