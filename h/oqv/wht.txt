Q: Vinificazione dei vini bianchi. || Vinification des vins blancs. || White winemaking.
P6: Spiega le scelte per un bianco fruttato aromatico (es. Sauvignon) rispetto a un bianco complesso e ricco (es. Chardonnay in legno). || Expliquez les choix pour un blanc fruité aromatique (ex. sauvignon) face à un blanc riche et complexe (ex. chardonnay boisé). || Explain the choices for a fruity aromatic white (e.g. Sauvignon) versus a rich, complex white (e.g. oaked Chardonnay).
K: aromatic (Sauvignon, Riesling, Muscat, Gewurztraminer, Torrontés): retain/enhance primary fruit; careful handling, SO2 monitored; crushed or whole bunches, maybe brief cool skin contact (hours) for intensity and texture; clean juice by gentle settling; inert vessels (stainless steel, e.g. NZ), cool fermentation (12–22°C range; too cold → pear drop) slow steady, primary aromas; MLF avoided (SO2) to keep acidity and avoid buttery notes masking fruit; little post-fermentation work, bottled early, no new oak (Alsace large old foudres for texture); non-aromatic (Chardonnay, Pinot Gris): enhance base material; whole-bunch pressing common for Chardonnay; controlled oxygen before fermentation may improve ageing; some solids left for complexity/texture; barrel fermentation (higher end of range), new oak barriques give toast and rounder texture (Côte d'Or), older/larger oak less flavour; MLF rounder creamier (almost all premium Burgundy incl. Chablis); extended fine lees contact and lees stirring for richness; blending batches with different lees/MLF/oak for complexity
P4: Quali tecniche si usano per i bianchi dolci (fermentazione arrestata, aggiunta di mosto, concentrazione)? || Quelles techniques pour les blancs doux (arrêt de fermentation, ajout de moût, concentration) ? || What techniques produce sweet white wines (stopping fermentation, adding sweet reserve, concentration)?
K: stopping fermentation: fortification (traditional, alters balance), high SO2 dose or chilling then filtering out yeast (German Kabinett, Spätlese, Asti) → low alcohol; adding sweetening component: Süssreserve (unfermented juice filtered or SO2-dosed, added before bottling, Germany), RCGM for a little sweetness in inexpensive wines; concentrating sugars: noble rot (Botrytis cinerea, fully ripe grapes, misty mornings then sunny dry afternoons; holes in skin, water evaporates, concentrates sugar, acid, flavour; honey, apricot, citrus zest, dried fruit; several hand pickings, not every year, grey rot risk; Sauternes, Tokaji, BA/TBA); passerillage (drying on vine, warm dry autumn, late harvest, over-ripe dried/tropical fruit); drying after picking (passito, Recioto della Valpolicella, raisiny; remove rotten grapes); freezing on vine (Eiswein, Icewine, pure varietal) or artificially; fermentation stops naturally, sometimes ~7% abv (TBA)

Q: Bianchi di grande volume e bianchi premium. || Blancs de grand volume et blancs haut de gamme. || High-volume and premium whites.
P5: Come si produce un bianco economico di grande volume (es. Chardonnay o Pinot Grigio di clima caldo)? || Comment produit-on un blanc bon marché de grand volume (ex. chardonnay ou pinot grigio de climat chaud) ? || How is a high-volume inexpensive white (e.g. warm-climate Chardonnay or Pinot Grigio) made?
K: neutral, easy-to-ripen varieties (Chardonnay, Pinot Grigio) appeal widely, often warm climates; varietal labelled or blends ('dry white'); protective handling, SO2 monitored; destem, crush, press; acidification common (low-acid Chardonnay flat, flabby); juice highly clarified by centrifuge or filter (settling too slow); stainless steel, cool fermentation to preserve primary fruit (vital for high-yield Pinot Grigio), commercial yeasts; MLF blocked (chilling, SO2) unless soft buttery Chardonnay wanted; racked off lees quickly; oak chips/staves for toast instead of barrels; residual sugar added after dry fermentation with juice or RCGM (precise control); stabilised, fined, sterile filtered; SO2 topped up at bottling; Pinot Grigio light pear drop, light body, medium acidity; Sauvignon high yields but herbaceous if underripe
P5: Confronta lo stile di Sauvignon Blanc e Chardonnay in diverse regioni secondo il capitolo. || Comparez le style du sauvignon blanc et du chardonnay selon les régions d'après le chapitre. || Compare Sauvignon Blanc and Chardonnay styles across regions according to the chapter.
K: Sauvignon: aromatic, high acidity, early ripening, suits cool climates; Sancerre/Pouilly-Fumé elegant, restrained, green apple, asparagus, wet pebbles; Marlborough cool but intense sunlight → gooseberry, elderflower, grapefruit, passion fruit, herbaceous; South Africa, Chile; Margaret River blended with Semillon (fuller, less aromatic); drink young; Pessac-Léognan / Californian Fumé Blanc: barrel fermentation, ambient yeast, lees, MLF, new oak, blended with Semillon, rounder, spicy; Chardonnay: early budding (frost), ripens in many climates; cool → green fruit, citrus; moderate → white peach, melon; warm/hot → banana, pineapple, loses acid quickly (harvest timing); Chablis high acid, green apple, citrus, wet stones; Côte d'Or stone fruit with creamy oak; Mâconnais riper, rounder; nuts/mushroom with age; New World less heavily oaked now (Russian River, Carneros, Adelaide Hills, Geelong, Mornington, Gisborne, Marlborough, Casablanca)

Q: Riesling e Pinot Gris/Grigio. || Riesling et pinot gris/grigio. || Riesling and Pinot Gris/Grigio.
P5: Perché il Riesling si adatta ai climi freschi e a diversi livelli di dolcezza, e come evolve? || Pourquoi le riesling convient-il aux climats frais et à divers niveaux de douceur, et comment évolue-t-il ? || Why does Riesling suit cool climates and a range of sweetness levels, and how does it age?
K: tolerant of cold winters, late budding avoids spring frost → cool climates: green fruit, floral; warmer: richer citrus, stone fruit, less delicate; mid-to-late ripening, accumulates sugar without losing high acidity → balanced in many sweetness levels, also botrytised; very long-lived, stays fresh; honey, toast, sometimes petrol; Germany homeland, sweetness balanced by acidity; Alsace mainly dry (late harvest/botrytis when vintage permits); Austria dry or very sweet; Clare/Eden bone dry, lime; Washington dry; NZ and Finger Lakes off-dry; rarely blended; sweet premium versions by stopping fermentation (chilling/SO2), botrytised stop naturally
P5: Confronta Pinot Gris d'Alsazia e Nuova Zelanda con Pinot Grigio del Nord-Est italiano e del Veneto. || Comparez pinot gris d'Alsace et de Nouvelle-Zélande avec pinot grigio du nord-est italien et de Vénétie. || Compare Pinot Gris from Alsace and New Zealand with Pinot Grigio from north-east Italy and the Veneto.
K: early budding, early ripening; in warm climates/late picking high sugar, loses acid; Alsace: dry or off-dry, oily texture, ripe tropical fruit, ginger, honey, deep-coloured skins can give golden wines, large old oak, fermentation may stop naturally leaving sugar; NZ Pinot Gris: viscous, medium acid, purer fruit, often more residual sugar (stopped by chilling or SO2); Tasmania, Oregon rich, often dry; Alto Adige, Trentino, Friuli: dry, more depth, small-berried French/German clones, concentration, stainless steel; Veneto plain: pale-skinned large fleshy clone, high yields, early harvest → neutral volume wines
