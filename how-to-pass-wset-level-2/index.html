How to pass WSET Level 2 first time

In short

To pass WSET Level 2 you need 55% in a 50-question multiple-choice exam. Study a little every day for about three weeks: learn the principal grapes with their regions and styles, understand the cause and effect of climate and winemaking, and finish with timed practice tests.

Updated 2026-11-15 · The Wine Goat

Level 2 is very passable with steady preparation. The exam rewards understanding why wines taste as they do, not memorising lists.

The exam in brief

  • 50 multiple-choice questions in one hour.
  • Pass mark 55%; higher scores give Merit or Distinction.
  • No tasting exam and no written answers.

A 3-week plan

WeekFocusDaily practice
1Vineyard and winery: climate, ripening, oak, malolactic, lees15 questions + 1 summary card
2Principal grapes with their regions and styles20 mixed grape ↔ region questions
3Sparkling, sweet and fortified wines, labels, food pairing; full mock testsOne timed 50-question test every two days

Common traps

  • Options that pair a real grape with the wrong region.
  • Confusing cool-climate and warm-climate styles of the same grape.
  • Label terms (Riserva, Reserva, Spätlese, Crianza) mixed up between countries.
  • Reading too fast: underline the key word (most, least, NOT).

Frequently asked questions

How hard is WSET Level 2?

It is a moderate step: most candidates who follow the course and practise regularly pass.

How long should I study for WSET Level 2?

Around three weeks of short daily sessions on top of the course is a comfortable plan for most people.

Can I take WSET Level 2 without Level 1?

Yes: Level 1 is not required for Level 2.
