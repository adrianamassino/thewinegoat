How to pass WSET Level 3 first time: study plan and tips

The WSET Level 3 Award in Wines is a demanding exam: the whole wine world in one syllabus, 50 multiple-choice questions, 4 short written answers and a blind tasting of two wines, with 55% needed in each part. Candidates who pass first time usually do three things: they study a little every day, they practise exam questions from the start, and they train the written answers on paper.

The Wine Goat builds that routine for you. Tell the app your goal (Pass, Merit or Distinction), your exam date, how much time you have each day and your course days: it creates a day-by-day study plan, tracks your mastery and keeps showing you your weak points until they are gone.

  • Start revising in week one of your course, not after it.
  • Follow a daily plan with a fixed amount of questions and a weekly mock exam.
  • Write a short answer on paper every week and mark it against the key points.
  • Taste with the SAT as often as you can, writing full notes.
  • Spend the last two weeks on mock exams and your weak topics.

Sample questions with answers

1 Which two food components make wine taste 'harder'?

Show the answer

Answer: Sweetness and umami

Why: Sweetness and umami make wine more astringent, bitter and acidic, less sweet and fruity.

2 At what temperature should wine be stored long term?

Show the answer

Answer: Cool and constant, 10–15 °C, in the dark

Why: Extremes of heat and cold damage wine; constancy matters most.

3 What is usually the largest production cost in an established vineyard?

Show the answer

Answer: Labour

Why: It is cut with seasonal workers or mechanisation; not possible on isolated or very steep sites.

Frequently asked questions

How hard is WSET Level 3?

It is a big step up from Level 2: the syllabus covers the whole wine world in depth and the written answers require explanations, not lists. Regular practice makes the difference.

How many hours should I study for WSET Level 3?

Beyond the course hours, most candidates need many hours of personal study; a steady daily routine over several weeks works better than cramming.

Can I get a Merit or Distinction at WSET 3?

Yes: results are Pass, Merit or Distinction. Set your goal in The Wine Goat and the plan adapts the number of questions and written answers to reach it.
