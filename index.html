Frequently asked questions

Is The Wine Goat the official WSET website?

No. The Wine Goat is an independent exam preparation platform. It is not affiliated with or approved by the Wine & Spirit Education Trust: to sit the exam you need to enrol with an Approved Programme Provider.

How do the modules work?

Each module covers part of the syllabus (for example Europe or The winery) with practice questions, marked open questions, games and revision material. You unlock it with a one-off payment and keep it for 12 months, with no automatic renewal.

What can I use for free?

The whole Bordeaux module, in full, plus a few trial exercises from the other modules. No credit card needed.

Can The Wine Goat replace the WSET Level 3 course?

No, it complements it. The course and official materials give you the knowledge; The Wine Goat trains you to recall it and write it well on exam day.

Which languages is it available in?

English, Italian and French, and you can switch at any time: useful if you study in your own language but sit the exam in English.

How does marking of short written answers work?

Type your answer: the app compares it with the textbook key points, shows which points you hit and which are missing, estimates your score and shows you the model answer. It all happens on your device, with no external service.

How long does it take to prepare for WSET Level 3?

It depends on your starting point, but most students need a few months of regular study on top of course hours. Modules stay yours for 12 months, so there is time even for a resit.
