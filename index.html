And one short written answer

In the WSET 3 exam, open questions are worth half of the theory marks. Answer in your head, then reveal the points that earn marks.

Wine B has a natural cork closure. What storage conditions would you recommend for keeping this wine long term? (5 marks)

Show the points to make
  • Lying on its side so the cork stays moist and does not dry out and let in oxygen
  • cool, constant temperature (about 10–15 °C) without fluctuations that expand the wine and push the cork
  • in the dark (light, especially UV, damages wine)
  • moderate humidity
  • away from vibration and strong smells.

How the WSET Level 3 exam works

Knowing how you are assessed is half the preparation. The WSET Level 3 Award in Wines exam has two parts, and you must pass both.

Theory: 50 multiple-choice questions

They cover the whole syllabus, from vineyard conditions to the world's regions. Speed and accuracy matter.

Theory: 4 short written answer questions

Written questions split into sub-questions: you score by linking cause and effect, not by listing keywords.

Blind tasting of 2 wines

A full tasting note using the Systematic Approach to Tasting (SAT): accurate description and reasoned conclusions.

Pass mark: 55%

You need 55% in the tasting and in each of the two theory sections. Results are Pass, Merit or Distinction.

The Wine Goat tip: short written answers are where most marks are lost. Practise them on paper from week one.

How to prepare for the WSET Level 3 exam

The guide to passing the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines first time: what to study, how to practise and which mistakes to avoid.

WSET 3 multiple-choice practice questions

The first theory section has 50 multiple-choice questions. The most effective preparation is answering lots of exam-style questions and understanding every mistake: The Wine Goat has 2,000+ WSET Level 3 practice questions with explanations, organised by topic and region, from vineyard to winery, from Bordeaux to New Zealand.

WSET 3 short written answers

The 4 short written answer questions are worth half of the theory marks, and they are where most marks are lost. Knowing facts is not enough: you have to explain why, linking cause and effect (climate → ripeness → wine style). With The Wine Goat you type your answer, get point-by-point marking against the textbook key points and see the model answer.

WSET 3 mock exams

Sitting a few full timed mock exams is the best way to walk in with confidence: you get used to the pace and see which chapters need revision. The Exam and strategy module includes WSET Level 3 mock exams, exam logic and the most common traps.

WSET 3 tasting with the SAT

The blind tasting of two wines is prepared with the Systematic Approach to Tasting (SAT): appearance, nose, palate and conclusions on quality and readiness for drinking. The app gives you the vocabulary for every heading and questions to train your conclusions; the glass remains the best practice.

Most revision material exists only in English. The Wine Goat is in English, Italian and French: study in the language you think in and switch to English in one tap before the exam.

WSET Level 3 exam prep: frequently asked questions

Is The Wine Goat the official WSET website?

No. The Wine Goat is an independent exam preparation platform. It is not affiliated with or approved by the Wine & Spirit Education Trust: to sit the exam you need to enrol with an Approved Programme Provider.

How do the modules work?

Each module covers part of the syllabus (for example Europe or The winery) with practice questions, marked open questions, games and revision material. You unlock it with a one-off payment and keep it for 12 months, with no automatic renewal.

What can I use for free?

The whole Bordeaux module, in full, plus a few trial exercises from the other modules. No credit card needed.

Can The Wine Goat replace the WSET Level 3 course?

No, it complements it. The course and official materials give you the knowledge; The Wine Goat trains you to recall it and write it well on exam day.

Which languages is it available in?

English, Italian and French, and you can switch at any time: useful if you study in your own language but sit the exam in English.

How does marking of short written answers work?

Type your answer: the app compares it with the textbook key points, shows which points you hit and which are missing, estimates your score and shows you the model answer. It all happens on your device, with no external service.

How long does it take to prepare for WSET Level 3?

It depends on your starting point, but most students need a few months of regular study on top of course hours. Modules stay yours for 12 months, so there is time even for a resit.

How many questions are in the WSET Level 3 exam?

The theory paper has 50 multiple-choice questions and 4 short written answer questions, followed by a blind tasting of two wines.

What is the pass mark for WSET Level 3?

55% in the tasting and in each of the two theory sections. Results are Pass, Merit or Distinction.

Can I practise tasting too?

Yes: a guide to the SAT, the vocabulary for every heading and questions on quality and readiness for drinking. Nothing replaces the wine in your glass, though.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, The Wine Goat runs in the browser on phone, tablet and computer. Your progress is saved on the device you use.

Will there be WSET Level 1 and Level 2 prep?

Yes, Level 2 and Level 1 are coming with the same method and the same module-based pricing.
