Frequently asked questions

Is The Wine Goat affiliated with WSET?

No. The Wine Goat is independent and not endorsed by WSET®. Course providers should check that recommending a third-party revision tool fits their own agreement.

Does it replace our course?

No. It is a revision and practice tool between lessons and before the exam. Your teaching, tastings and materials stay at the centre.

Can we try it with one class?

Yes. We offer a free pilot for one class so you can see engagement and results before deciding.

Which levels are covered?

Level 3 in full today. Levels 1 and 2 are coming soon and will be included in licences at no extra cost when they launch.

How are students’ data handled?

Accounts are personal and data are stored in the EU-compliant infrastructure we use; we only share aggregated results with the school, with the students’ consent.
