## A · Same grape. Two wines.
Files: `posts/a-same-grape-two-wines/` (6 slides)

**Caption**
WINE · LEVEL 2
Same grape. Two completely different wines. 🍇
Climate decides how much sugar and acidity a grape keeps.
Cooler: less sugar, more acidity, so lighter and fresher wines.
Warmer: more sugar, less acidity and riper flavours.
Chardonnay: green apple, lemon and wet stones when cool; peach, pineapple and banana when warm.
Sauvignon Blanc: gooseberry and grapefruit when less ripe; peach and passion fruit when riper.
Swipe for the cheat sheet and the two questions to ask about any glass.

Which style do you reach for: cool or warm? 👇
