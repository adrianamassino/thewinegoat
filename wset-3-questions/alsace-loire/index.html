Sample questions with answers

1 What makes Alsace unusual among French regions?

Show the answer

Answer: Most of its wines are varietally labelled

Why: It focuses on aromatic whites, dry, off-dry and sweet; some red is made.

2 Where are the best Alsace vineyards?

Show the answer

Answer: On the steepest slopes facing east and south-east

Why: Plain vineyards often supply Crémant d'Alsace; the north is cooler and less sheltered.

3 What are the two main appellations for still Alsace wines?

Show the answer

Answer: Alsace and Alsace Grand Cru

Why: VT and SGN are ripeness-based terms for both. 'Réserve personnelle' and 'Cuvée spéciale' have no legal status.

4 If an Alsace wine names a grape on the label, what percentage must it contain?

Show the answer

Answer: 100%

Why: A few blends are made, which are not varietally labelled.

5 How many vineyards have their own Alsace Grand Cru appellation?

Show the answer

Answer: Over 50

Why: Labels show vineyard, vintage and variety. Controversy concerns boundaries seen as too large; some producers don't use the term.

6 How is the ripeness for SGN usually achieved?

Show the answer

Answer: With noble rot

Why: Thresholds exceed VT; SGN wines are always sweet and not made every year.
