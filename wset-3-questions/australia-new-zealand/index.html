Sample questions with answers

1 What is Australia's principal grape of either colour?

Show the answer

Answer: Shiraz

Why: Hunter and Barossa give full fruity Shiraz; Geelong and Heathcote leaner, peppery. With Cabernet it plays Merlot's Bordeaux role.

2 How does Australian Cabernet generally differ from Shiraz?

Show the answer

Answer: It is darker, with firmer tannin and higher acidity

Why: Ripe blackcurrant and black cherry, often toasty oak; classic regions Coonawarra and Margaret River. Merlot mostly in blends.

3 Where do Australia's best Pinot Noirs come from?

Show the answer

Answer: Yarra Valley, Mornington Peninsula and Tasmania

Why: Style: medium body and alcohol, medium-high acid, cherry and strawberry. Hot regions suit Grenache, Petit Verdot, Mataro.

4 What is the hierarchy of Australian GIs?

Show the answer

Answer: Zones, regions, subregions

Why: Zones are large areas; regions must be distinct. Eden Valley is in Barossa zone, in South Australia, in South Eastern Australia.

5 Which region is the benchmark for Australian Sauvignon Blanc?

Show the answer

Answer: Adelaide Hills

Why: Concentrated passion fruit and high acid. Chardonnay, the main white, ranges from chip-oaked blends to balanced cool-region wines.

6 What style does Riverina specialise in besides volume wines?

Show the answer

Answer: Botrytised Semillon, aided by autumn morning mists

Why: It is in New South Wales.
