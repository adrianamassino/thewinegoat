Sample questions with answers

1 What is Austria best known for?

Show the answer

Answer: Quality dry whites from Grüner Veltliner and Riesling

Why: It also makes fine reds (Zweigelt, Blaufränkisch) and botrytised Welschriesling sweet wines.

2 What climate do Austria's wine regions have?

Show the answer

Answer: Cool continental, with short summers and harsh winters

Why: The Alps cover the west, so vineyards are in the east. The north has cool winds, the Hungarian border warm easterlies.

3 What is Austria's most widely planted grape?

Show the answer

Answer: Grüner Veltliner

Why: At high yields it is unexciting; carefully grown it is full, concentrated, high-acid, citrus or stone fruit, sometimes white pepper, then honey and toast.

4 Is Welschriesling the same grape as Riesling?

Show the answer

Answer: No, it is a different variety

Why: It is Austria's second white: dry it is simple and acidic, but its botrytis susceptibility gives fine sweet wines.

5 Where do the best Austrian Rieslings come from?

Show the answer

Answer: Wachau, Kamptal, Kremstal

Why: Usually dry, medium to full, ripe peach; the best age in bottle.

6 In which Austrian region does Sauvignon Blanc have a growing reputation?

Show the answer

Answer: Styria

Why: It is a southern region; Chardonnay and Müller-Thurgau are also widely planted.
