Sample questions with answers

1 How late can spring frosts occur in Chablis?

Show the answer

Answer: As late as May

Why: Another hazard is localised summer hail, which can destroy much of a crop.

2 Where are premier and grand cru vineyards usually located in Burgundy?

Show the answer

Answer: Mid-slope, facing south or east

Why: Village sites are flat or low; mid-slope has less frost, thinner well-drained soils and shelter from westerly winds.

3 How does classic Burgundy Pinot Noir evolve with age?

Show the answer

Answer: From red fruit to earth, game and mushroom

Why: It usually has high acidity and low-medium tannin. Top wines get 16-18 months in barrel, part new.

4 What share of Burgundy's vineyards is Chardonnay?

Show the answer

Answer: Nearly half

Why: Pinot Noir covers over a third.

5 Which premium Chardonnay techniques did Burgundy pioneer?

Show the answer

Answer: Barrel fermentation and ageing, MLF and lees use

Why: Barrel ageing is typically 6-9 months; the best mature for a decade or more.

6 What wines does Aligoté usually make?

Show the answer

Answer: Neutral whites with high acidity

Why: It can be high quality where fully ripe, but those sites go to more profitable Pinot and Chardonnay.
