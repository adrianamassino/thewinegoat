WSET Level 3 Chile and Argentina: practice questions with answers

Sample questions with answers

1 What are the four key geographical features of Chile's vineyards, west to east?

Show the answer

Answer: Pacific Ocean, Coastal Range, Central Valley, Andes

Why: Vineyards span over 900 km north-south but rarely 100 km east-west.

2 What effects do El Niño and La Niña have in Chile?

Show the answer

Answer: El Niño can sharply increase rain; La Niña may bring drought

Why: Chile is broadly warm Mediterranean, dry and sunny with little disease; irrigation water is increasingly scarce.

3 What is Chile's most planted grape of either colour?

Show the answer

Answer: Cabernet

Why: From simple to premium; very ripe black fruit, often herbaceous; blended with Merlot, Carmenère or Syrah.

4 Why does Carmenère do best in the warmest, sunniest sites?

Show the answer

Answer: It ripens late; picked unripe, it is markedly herbaceous

Why: Full, often tannic; good ones balance black fruit and herbs. Seen as Chile's signature grape.

5 Which Chilean valley produces particularly structured, elegant, restrained Chardonnay?

Show the answer

Answer: Limarí

Why: Most are modern international style; Aconcagua and Casablanca also make fine wines.

6 What is Muscat of Alexandria mainly used for in Chile?

Show the answer

Answer: The grape brandy Pisco

Why: It is the third most planted white. Aromatic grapes do well in cool Bío Bío and Itata.
