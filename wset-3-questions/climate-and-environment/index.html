WSET Level 3 Climate and environment: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on Climate and environment, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 94 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 What five things does the vine need?

Show the answer

Answer: Heat, light, water, nutrients, CO2

Why: CO2 is always plentiful; heat, light, water and nutrients vary and affect yield and quality.

2 Below what temperature is it too cold for the vine to grow?

Show the answer

Answer: 10 °C

Why: That is why vines are dormant in winter and bud in spring.

3 What factor determines which varieties can be grown where?

Show the answer

Answer: The amount of heat in the growing season

Why: For example, Riesling thrives in cool places where Grenache would not ripen.

4 Between which latitudes do most vineyards lie?

Show the answer

Answer: 30° and 50° north and south

Why: Nearer the Equator is too hot, nearer the Poles too cold; other factors can make areas outside suitable.

5 In which area does altitude offset closeness to the Equator?

Show the answer

Answer: Cafayate, northern Argentina

Why: Temperature drops with altitude, so areas near the Equator can grow vines.

6 Which cold ocean currents influence vineyards?

Show the answer

Answer: Humboldt (Chile) and Benguela (South Africa)

Why: They cool otherwise hot regions; the Gulf Stream instead warms north-west Europe.
