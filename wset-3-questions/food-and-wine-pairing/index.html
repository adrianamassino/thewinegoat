WSET Level 3 Food and wine pairing: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on Food and wine pairing, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 57 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 Which two food components make wine taste 'harder'?

Show the answer

Answer: Sweetness and umami

Why: Sweetness and umami make wine more astringent, bitter and acidic, less sweet and fruity.

2 Which two food components make wine taste 'softer'?

Show the answer

Answer: Salt and acidity

Why: Salt and acid make wine less astringent, bitter and acidic, sweeter and fruitier.

3 Generally, which has more impact on the other?

Show the answer

Answer: Food on wine, and more often in a negative way

Why: Food changes wine more than vice versa, mostly for the worse.

4 What simple experiment lets you taste umami?

Show the answer

Answer: Compare a raw mushroom with one microwaved for 30 seconds

Why: Cooking greatly increases umami. Umami usually appears with other tastes, e.g. with salt in MSG.

5 Which foods are high in umami but lack salt, so are hard to pair?

Show the answer

Answer: Asparagus, eggs, mushrooms, ripe soft cheeses

Why: Cured or smoked foods and hard cheeses like Parmesan are also salty, so harm wine less.

6 Which wines can become surprisingly bitter with umami-rich foods?

Show the answer

Answer: Low-tannin reds and whites with oak or skin contact

Why: In a balanced high-tannin red the extra bitterness does not unbalance it; in low-tannin wines it stands out.
