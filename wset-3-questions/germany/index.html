WSET Level 3 Germany: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on Germany, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 67 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 What climate do most German wine regions have?

Show the answer

Answer: Cool continental

Why: Baden in the south is noticeably warmer. The long cool ripening gives sugar while retaining acidity.

2 How are vines trained on Germany's steepest slopes?

Show the answer

Answer: Head-pruned, individually staked, canes tied at the top of the stake

Why: This maximises light exposure and air circulation.

3 What is Germany's most widely planted grape?

Show the answer

Answer: Riesling

Why: It ranges from dry to luscious, floral to richer peach; the best dry ones are often Grosses Gewächs.

4 How should the Prädikat categories be understood?

Show the answer

Answer: As an indicator of style

Why: Nearly all have residual sugar, but up to Auslese they can be dry.

5 What alcohol does a sweet Kabinett usually have?

Show the answer

Answer: 8-9% abv

Why: Drier ones reach 12%. They are the most delicate Prädikate: light, high-acid, green apple and citrus.

6 How does Spätlese differ from Kabinett from the same region?

Show the answer

Answer: More concentrated and riper, a little more body, alcohol and sweetness

Why: It is made like Kabinett; Riesling Spätlese often shows citrus and stone fruit.
