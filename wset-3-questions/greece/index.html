Sample questions with answers

1 Which three Greek grapes are most recognised abroad, and from where?

Show the answer

Answer: Xinomavro (Naoussa), Agiorgitiko (Nemea), Assyrtiko (Santorini)

Why: Greece has over 200 native varieties suited to heat; most PDO wines require native grapes.

2 Which grape makes Naoussa PDO?

Show the answer

Answer: Exclusively Xinomavro

Why: Naoussa is in Macedonia, vineyards up to 400 m, cooler than surroundings. It is a red-wine PDO.

3 Which grape is Xinomavro often compared with?

Show the answer

Answer: Nebbiolo

Why: High tannin and acidity, medium colour fading quickly to tawny, little fresh fruit; long-lived, developing spice and earth.

4 Where is Nemea and which grape is used?

Show the answer

Answer: In the northern Peloponnese, Agiorgitiko only

Why: Vineyards range 230-900 m; the best are mid-range. Low sites are jammy; high sites more acidic, best for rosé.

5 Greece: appellation / grape

Show the answer

Answer: Naoussa / Xinomavro

Why: Naoussa = Xinomavro; Nemea = Agiorgitiko; Santorini = Assyrtiko.

6 Even in the wetter west of Greece, why can vines suffer water stress?

Show the answer

Answer: Because there is almost no rain in the growing season

Why: Mediterranean climate: rain falls in winter, almost none in summer.
