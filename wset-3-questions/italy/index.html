WSET Level 3 Italy: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on Italy, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 102 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 Which traditional Italian term corresponds to PGI?

Show the answer

Answer: IGT (Tipica)

Why: The PDO term is DOP, but DOC and DOCG are more widely seen.

2 Why does Northern Italy have dry, short summers?

Show the answer

Answer: The Alps form a barrier, sheltering vineyards from rain

Why: Rivers like the Po and lakes like Garda moderate inland areas; near the sea more rain brings fungal disease.

3 What is Italy's most northerly wine region?

Show the answer

Answer: Alto Adige

Why: Around Bolzano, on terraces along the Adige; the wide diurnal range suits aromatic whites and elegant light reds.

4 Which grape dominates Alto Adige reds?

Show the answer

Answer: Schiava

Why: It gives light, fruity wines with raspberry and plum. The key white is dry, high-acid Pinot Grigio.

5 Which of these Italian varieties is a black grape?

Show the answer

Answer: Dolcetto

Why: Dolcetto: black, Piedmont. Cortese (Gavi), Grechetto (Orvieto), Verdicchio (Marche): white.

6 Which of these wines is NOT made from Nebbiolo?

Show the answer

Answer: Brunello

Why: Brunello = Sangiovese (Tuscany).
