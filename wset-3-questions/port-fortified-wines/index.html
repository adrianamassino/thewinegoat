Sample questions with answers

1 What are the Douro's three Port sub-regions?

Show the answer

Answer: Baixo Corgo, Cima Corgo, Douro Superior

Why: Baixo Corgo is coolest and wettest with the lightest wines; Cima Corgo has most top vineyards; Douro Superior is renowned. Hotter and drier eastward.

2 What shields the Douro from rain-bearing Atlantic winds?

Show the answer

Answer: The Serra do Marão

Why: The climate is warm continental; vineyards start 70 km upstream of Porto and Gaia.

3 At what alcohol level is Port fermentation stopped?

Show the answer

Answer: 5–9% abv, after about 24–36 hours

Why: The time is so short that normal extraction is too weak: foot treading, autovinifiers, pistons and robotic lagares are used.

4 What is a lagar?

Show the answer

Answer: A shallow granite trough for treading grapes by foot

Why: The benchmark for other methods; labour-intensive, still used for some premium wines.

5 Which is the most widely planted grape in the Douro?

Show the answer

Answer: Touriga Franca

Why: Most planted: Touriga Franca. Most prized: Touriga Nacional (low yields).

6 What strength is Port after fortification?

Show the answer

Answer: 19-22% abv

Why: Fortification kills yeast; timing depends on must weight and desired sweetness.
