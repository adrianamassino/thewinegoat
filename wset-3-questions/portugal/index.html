WSET Level 3 Portugal: practice questions with answers

Sample questions with answers

1 What is the single most important climatic influence in Portugal?

Show the answer

Answer: The Atlantic Ocean

Why: Much is maritime, but many inland vineyards are hot, dry and continental; altitude mitigates heat in places.

2 What is the traditional Portuguese PGI term still seen on labels?

Show the answer

Answer: Vinho Regional

Why: PDO = DOP (traditionally DOC); PGI = IGP. Wines without GI are 'Vinho', including successful simple off-dry rosés.

3 What is Vinho Verde's most significant climatic feature?

Show the answer

Answer: Abundant rainfall

Why: Without canopy management rain brings excess growth and disease; pergolas have given way to spur-pruned VSP for airflow and mechanisation.

4 What is classic white Vinho Verde like?

Show the answer

Answer: Pale lemon, high acidity, low alcohol, often slightly spritzy

Why: Lower-alcohol ones are usually off-dry. Loureiro and Arinto. With a variety or sub-region named, up to 14%.

5 Where does Vinho Verde Alvarinho come from and what is it like?

Show the answer

Answer: From Monção e Melgaço; 11.5–14% with riper, tropical aromas

Why: 100% Alvarinho (Albariño in Spain). Vinho Verde reds are deep and tannic, rosés fresh and fruity.

6 Which local grapes are used for white Vinho Verde?

Show the answer

Answer: Loureiro and Arinto

Why: Vinho Verde: Loureiro, Arinto; Alvarinho for Monção e Melgaço.
