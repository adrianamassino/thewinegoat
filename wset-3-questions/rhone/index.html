Sample questions with answers

1 Between which towns are the Northern Rhône vineyards?

Show the answer

Answer: Between Vienne in the north and Valence in the south

Why: They are a small share of Rhône production but home to many premium wines.

2 What do the Northern Rhône's lateral valleys protect against?

Show the answer

Answer: The mistral, a cold north wind that can damage vines

Why: The best sites are steep south-facing slopes; vines are on single stakes or tepee arrangements.

3 What climate does the Northern Rhône have?

Show the answer

Answer: Moderate continental, similar to Beaujolais

Why: It is noticeably cooler than the Southern Rhône.

4 What is the only black grape permitted in the Northern Rhône?

Show the answer

Answer: Syrah

Why: It is at its northern ripening limit; prized sites are very steep and south-facing. Deep wines, medium-high tannin, black fruit, sometimes pepper or floral.

5 How are vines supported on the steep Northern Rhône slopes?

Show the answer

Answer: With an individual stake or a tepee of stakes

Why: Northern Rhône: steep south-facing slopes by the river, lateral valleys sheltered from the mistral; individual stakes.

6 What do Marsanne and Roussanne contribute in a blend?

Show the answer

Answer: Marsanne richness and weight, Roussanne acidity and perfumed fruit

Why: Less aromatic than Viognier, they age well, developing hazelnut aromas.
