Sample questions with answers

1 In which towns must Sherry be matured?

Show the answer

Answer: Jerez, Sanlúcar or El Puerto

Why: Current rule: Jerez, Sanlúcar or El Puerto. A reform awaiting EU approval would allow ageing anywhere in the DO, except Fino in Sanlúcar (that is Manzanilla).

2 Where does most PX used for Sherry come from?

Show the answer

Answer: The neighbouring Montilla-Moriles region

Why: Its thin skin suits sun-drying; used for sweet Sherry. Little is planted in Jerez.

3 To what strength are wines for biological ageing fortified?

Show the answer

Answer: 15–15.5%, ideal for flor

Why: 95% neutral spirit is used. Oxidative wines go to 17%, where flor dies.

4 Why are Sherry butts only five-sixths full?

Show the answer

Answer: To encourage contact with oxygen and leave room for flor

Why: 600 L oak butts, often seasoned to remove oak flavour. Bodegas have thick walls, high ceilings and damp floors.

5 Which of these grapes are generally used for Sherry? 1. Muscat 2. Palomino 3. Pedro Ximénez (PX)

Show the answer

Answer: 1, 2 and 3

Why: Sherry: Palomino (base), PX and Moscatel (sweet styles).

6 Which of these statements is true?

Show the answer

Answer: Flor cannot develop if a wine is fortified to 17% abv

Why: Flor: 15–15.5% abv yes, 17% no. Oloroso = oxidative.
