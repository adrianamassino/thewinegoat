WSET Level 3 South Africa: practice questions with answers

Sample questions with answers

1 Why are vines planted on southern mountain slopes in South Africa?

Show the answer

Answer: In the southern hemisphere, south slopes are cooler

Why: Mountains offer varied altitudes, aspects and soils, cast shade and channel winds.

2 What is South Africa's most planted black grape?

Show the answer

Answer: Cabernet

Why: Varietal or in Bordeaux blends. Syrah is widely planted; Pinot Noir only in the coolest coastal areas.

3 What is Pinotage?

Show the answer

Answer: A Pinot Noir × Cinsault crossing developed in South Africa

Why: Barely grown elsewhere, it is a point of difference. Blended with international grapes: a 'Cape blend'.

4 What is a 'Cape blend'?

Show the answer

Answer: A blend of Pinotage with international grapes

Why: Cape blend = Pinotage + international varieties; made in Stellenbosch and Swartland.

5 Which are the most important wine-growing regions of the Western Cape?

Show the answer

Answer: Coastal Region, Breede River Valley and Cape South Coast

Why: Regions: Coastal Region, Breede River Valley, Cape South Coast; then districts and wards.

6 A South African Syrah is medium-bodied with peppery notes. What kind of area does it most likely come from?

Show the answer

Answer: From a cooler, often coastal area

Why: South African Syrah: hot = full, alcoholic, black fruit, sometimes meaty; cool = lighter, peppery.
