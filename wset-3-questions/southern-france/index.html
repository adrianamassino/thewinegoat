WSET Level 3 Southern France: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on Southern France, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 48 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 What are Southern France's three broad areas?

Show the answer

Answer: Languedoc, Roussillon and Provence

Why: Languedoc and Roussillon lie west of the Rhône, Provence east.

2 What is the biggest grape-growing challenge in Southern France?

Show the answer

Answer: Summer drought

Why: Fungal disease is less of a problem than elsewhere in France; occasional summer floods occur.

3 Which wind blows between the Massif Central and the Pyrenees?

Show the answer

Answer: Tramontane

Why: The Mistral blows down the Rhône; both are cool, dry northerly winds.

4 Why are inland foothill vineyards often better for quality?

Show the answer

Answer: They are cooler, with poor, well-drained soils

Why: The coastal plain is hotter and more fertile, suited to volume.

5 What are Carignan's characteristics?

Show the answer

Answer: High tannin, acidity and colour, but little fruit or finesse

Why: It was popular for huge yields; old vines on poor soils can make quality wine. All ACs permit it.

6 Which statement about Southern France is true?

Show the answer

Answer: IGP Pays d'Oc allows varietal wines from many grapes

Why: Pays d'Oc: flexible varietal IGP; Languedoc Mediterranean; Provence = rosé.
