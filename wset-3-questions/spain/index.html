WSET Level 3 Spain: practice questions with answers

Sample questions with answers

1 How are inexpensive Spanish reds often made to enhance fruit?

Show the answer

Answer: Unoaked, with semi-carbonic maceration

Why: The best almost always see oak, partly new; traditionally American, now French too.

2 Where does Garnacha give intense, full reds from low-yielding old vines?

Show the answer

Answer: Priorat

Why: Garnacha generally gives high-alcohol, early-maturing wines and is widely used for rosados.

3 What wine does Monastrell make in Jumilla and Yecla?

Show the answer

Answer: Very dark and full, high tannin and alcohol, low acid

Why: Monastrell (Mourvèdre) is thick-skinned and drought-tolerant but needs heat and sun.

4 What is Cariñena (Carignan) called in Rioja?

Show the answer

Answer: Mazuelo

Why: It gives high acid, tannin and colour; a little suits Tempranillo, and in Priorat it adds structure to Garnacha.

5 Which Spanish black grape, suited to moderate climates, is rising in prominence?

Show the answer

Answer: Mencía

Why: It gives fresh fruit, medium-high acidity, sometimes herbaceousness; its home is Bierzo.

6 How many Spanish DOCas are there, and which?

Show the answer

Answer: Two: Rioja and Priorat (DOQ in Catalan)

Why: A DO of at least ten years' standing may apply. PGI is Vino de la Tierra; others are simply 'Vino'.
