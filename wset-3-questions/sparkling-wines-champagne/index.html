Sample questions with answers

1 By how much does the second fermentation raise a sparkling wine's alcohol?

Show the answer

Answer: About 1.2-1.3%

Why: So the aim is a dry 10-11% base wine from low-sugar, high-acid grapes with ripe, non-green flavours.

2 What does liqueur de tirage contain?

Show the answer

Answer: Wine, sugar, yeast, yeast nutrients and a clarifying agent

Why: Sealed with a crown cap and plastic insert; slow cool fermentation creates 5-6 atmospheres.

3 What does yeast autolysis give and how long does it usually last?

Show the answer

Answer: Bread, biscuit and toast; usually 4–5 years (up to 10)

Why: After autolysis, keeping lees contact maintains freshness for years.

4 Grapes for premium sparkling wine often show: sugar / acidity

Show the answer

Answer: Low / high

Why: Base wine: just-ripe grapes, low sugar (10–11% abv) and high acidity.

5 Champagne: grape / contribution

Show the answer

Answer: Meunier / early fruitiness, more frost-resistant

Why: Chardonnay: citrus, finesse (Côte des Blancs); Pinot Noir: body, red fruit (Montagne); Meunier: fruit, frost (Marne).

6 Which grapes are used for a 'Blanc de Noirs' Champagne?

Show the answer

Answer: Black grapes only: Pinot Noir and/or Meunier

Why: Blanc de Noirs = white wine from black grapes; Blanc de Blancs = Chardonnay only.
