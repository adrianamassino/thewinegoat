WSET Level 3 Storage and service: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on Storage and service, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 49 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 At what temperature should wine be stored long term?

Show the answer

Answer: Cool and constant, 10–15 °C, in the dark

Why: Extremes of heat and cold damage wine; constancy matters most.

2 Why is a kitchen one of the worst places to store wine?

Show the answer

Answer: Because of wide temperature fluctuations

Why: Experts name temperature swings as the kitchen's main problem.

3 What can extended refrigeration cause?

Show the answer

Answer: Corks harden, lose their elasticity and start to let air in

Why: The seal fails, air makes the wine stale and sparkling wines lose their fizz.

4 Why are cork-sealed bottles stored on their side?

Show the answer

Answer: So the cork stays in contact with the wine and doesn't dry

Why: A dry cork lets in air that oxidises the wine. Screwcapped bottles can stand upright.

5 What is the service temperature for medium/full-bodied oaked whites (e.g. white Burgundy)?

Show the answer

Answer: 10–13 °C, lightly chilled

Why: Fumé Blanc is 's other example of this style.

6 What service temperature for light whites like Muscadet, Pinot Grigio, NZ Sauvignon Blanc?

Show the answer

Answer: 7–10 °C, chilled

Why: Experts also put Fino Sherry in this group.
