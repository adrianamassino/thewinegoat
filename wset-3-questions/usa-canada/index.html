WSET Level 3 USA and Canada: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on USA and Canada, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 75 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 What may appear on the label of a US wine outside any AVA?

Show the answer

Answer: A state (e.g. California) or county (e.g. Napa County) name

Why: Federal laws set minimum percentages for variety, vintage and AVA; many states have their own laws. Non-AVA rules are often more lenient.

2 Why does cooling reach far inland in valleys like Monterey and Santa Barbara?

Show the answer

Answer: They lie at right angles to the ocean and funnel cold air

Why: Without ocean exposure, as in the Central Valley, it is hot.

3 What is California's most planted black grape?

Show the answer

Answer: Cabernet Sauvignon

Why: Many are full with concentrated ripe cassis and new-oak spice; some are fresher.

4 What is White Zinfandel?

Show the answer

Answer: A rosé from early-picked grapes: pale, fruity, medium-sweet

Why: It is made in large volumes.

5 Where do better-quality Californian Merlots come from?

Show the answer

Answer: Cooler sites: Monterey, Napa, Sonoma

Why: San Joaquin has most plantings for simple wines. The best: deep, velvety tannin, high alcohol, blackberry and plum.

6 Which moderate Californian areas produce premium Pinot Noir?

Show the answer

Answer: Russian River, Los Carneros, Santa Maria

Why: Most show layers of ripe red fruit and sweet oak spice; some are light with gamey, vegetal notes.
