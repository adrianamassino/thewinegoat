WSET Level 3 The vine and grape varieties: practice questions with answers

Sample questions with answers

1 Which vine species produces nearly all wine grapes?

Show the answer

Answer: Vitis vinifera

Why: V. vinifera is the main Eurasian species, used to make wine for several thousand years.

2 Why are American vines important even though their wines are not liked?

Show the answer

Answer: They resist phylloxera and are used as rootstocks

Why: There are three important American species; vinifera is grafted onto their roots.

3 Why is a grape variety not propagated from seed?

Show the answer

Answer: A seedling is a new variety, different from its parents

Why: To keep a variety's qualities, cuttings or layering are used: the new plant is identical.

4 Why do most growers now use cuttings rather than layering?

Show the answer

Answer: Because of the risk of phylloxera

Why: Layering puts vinifera roots straight into the soil, exposed to phylloxera.

5 What is a clone?

Show the answer

Answer: One or more vines of a variety with unique traits from a mutation

Why: Clonal selection propagates vines with positive mutations; clones remain the same variety.

6 How is a genuinely new grape variety created?

Show the answer

Answer: By cross-fertilisation and planting the resulting seed

Why: Even with parents of the same variety, the seedling is a new, unpredictable variety.
