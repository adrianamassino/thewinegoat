Sample questions with answers

1 In which year after planting does the first crop usually come?

Show the answer

Answer: The third year

Why: Young pre-grafted vines are often protected by plastic sleeves; irrigation is sometimes allowed for establishment.

2 At what age are most vines replaced?

Show the answer

Answer: Between 30 and 50 years

Why: With age yields fall and disease susceptibility rises: quality must be balanced against profit.

3 How long is land usually left fallow after vines are dug up?

Show the answer

Answer: Three years or more

Why: Growers plan replanting cycles to keep as little land out of production as possible.

4 What does vine training refer to?

Show the answer

Answer: The shape of the vine's permanent wood

Why: It is split into head and cordon training; either can be low (soil heat) or high (avoiding frost).

5 What is replacement cane pruning with one or two canes also called?

Show the answer

Answer: Guyot (single or double)

Why: One cane in Single Guyot, two in Double. It is more complex than spur pruning and needs skilled labour.

6 Where are untrellised bush vines best suited?

Show the answer

Answer: In warm, dry and very sunny regions, often unirrigated

Why: The shade protects grapes; e.g. Southern Rhône, Barossa. They cannot be machine-harvested.
