WSET Level 3 Wine pricing and the trade: practice questions with answers

These are real questions from The Wine Goat bank on Wine pricing and the trade, written to the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines syllabus. Answer them, read the explanation, then practise the full set of 41 questions in the free app, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Sample questions with answers

1 What is usually the largest production cost in an established vineyard?

Show the answer

Answer: Labour

Why: It is cut with seasonal workers or mechanisation; not possible on isolated or very steep sites.

2 What is one way to reduce packaging and transport costs?

Show the answer

Answer: Shipping in bulk and bottling in the destination market

Why: Only large producers can do this, and local laws do not always permit it.

3 Why does wine cost more in restaurants than in shops?

Show the answer

Answer: Higher margins, covering occasion, setting and service

Why: Ultimately price depends on what the market will pay.

4 Why are co-operatives very common in Europe?

Show the answer

Answer: Average holdings are too small to make and sell wine alone

Why: Outside Europe they exist but are less common.

5 What does a merchant (négociant) do?

Show the answer

Answer: Buys grapes, juice or wine from growers or co-operatives

Why: Common especially outside Europe, where holdings are larger; many big brands are made this way.

6 What is the main disadvantage of an estate (domaine)?

Show the answer

Answer: Fewer blending options in difficult years

Why: An estate uses only its own grapes: maximum control and usually the best wines.
