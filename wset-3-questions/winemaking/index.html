WSET Level 3 Winemaking and maturation: practice questions with answers

Sample questions with answers

1 Which yeast carries the alcoholic fermentation of wine through to the end?

Show the answer

Answer: Saccharomyces cerevisiae

Why: Saccharomyces cerevisiae tolerates alcohol and finishes the fermentation.

2 Which microorganism is mainly responsible for malolactic conversion?

Show the answer

Answer: The lactic bacterium Oenococcus oeni

Why: Oenococcus oeni (sometimes Lactobacillus, Pediococcus): malic acid → lactic acid + CO₂.

3 What does a pre-fermentation cold soak (4-10°C) mainly extract?

Show the answer

Answer: Colour and fruity aromas, with few tannins

Why: Without alcohol, anthocyanins and aromas dissolve, not tannins; protected with SO₂ and dry ice.

4 In carbonic maceration, what produces the first 2 degrees of alcohol inside the whole berries?

Show the answer

Answer: The grape's own enzymes (intracellular fermentation)

Why: Without oxygen, the berry's enzymes make ~2% abv; yeasts finish after pressing.

5 Where does the CO₂ in wine mainly come from?

Show the answer

Answer: From the yeasts' alcoholic fermentation

Why: Sugars → alcohol + CO₂ + heat; a little from malolactic too.

6 What are the two main roles of SO₂ in wine?

Show the answer

Answer: Antimicrobial and antioxidant

Why: SO₂ holds back unwanted microbes and protects against oxidation.
