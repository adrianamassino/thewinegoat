WSET Level 1 study guide: everything in one page

In short

WSET Level 1 is a one-day beginner course ending with a short multiple-choice exam. To pass, know how wine is made, the main styles, about eight key grapes, how to store and serve wine and the basic food-pairing rules.

Updated 2026-11-15 · The Wine Goat

Level 1 is the gentlest step into wine: a short course and a multiple-choice exam. This page gathers what you need to know, so you can revise in an evening.

What the course covers

  • How wine is made: grapes, yeast, fermentation; white, rosé and red.
  • The main types and styles of wine: still, sparkling, fortified; dry to sweet; light to full.
  • Key grape varieties and their typical styles.
  • Storage and service: temperatures, glasses, opening a bottle.
  • Food and wine pairing: how food changes the taste of wine.

Five rules to remember

  • Sweet food makes wine taste drier, more acidic and bitter.
  • Salt and acidity in food make wine taste softer and fruitier.
  • Chilli heat increases the burning effect of alcohol.
  • Serve sparkling and sweet wines well chilled, light reds lightly chilled, full reds at room temperature.
  • Store bottles cool, dark and at a steady temperature, still wines with a cork on their side.

One-week study plan

  • Days 1–2: how wine is made and the styles.
  • Days 3–4: the key grapes.
  • Day 5: storage and service.
  • Day 6: food pairing.
  • Day 7: practice questions and review your mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the WSET Level 1 course?

It is usually a one-day course, followed by the exam the same day.

Is WSET Level 1 worth it?

Yes if you are new to wine or work in hospitality: it gives a clear, recognised foundation. If you already know the basics, Level 2 is more useful.

What do I need to memorise for WSET Level 1?

The main styles, the key grapes with their typical style, serving temperatures and the food-pairing rules. Short daily quizzes are enough.
