WSET Level 2 practice questions, with answers

The WSET Level 2 Award in Wines is assessed with a multiple-choice exam. The questions test whether you can explain why wines taste the way they do: grape, climate, winemaking and region.

Practise with the questions below, then train every day in the app with thousands more.

What the Level 2 exam looks like

  • 50 multiple-choice questions in one hour, no tasting exam.
  • You need 55% to pass; higher marks give a Merit or Distinction.
  • Most questions link a cause (climate, technique) to an effect on the wine.

How to prepare

  • Learn each key grape with its regions and typical style.
  • Understand the “why”: cool vs warm climate, oak, malolactic conversion, lees.
  • Do short timed quizzes daily and review every mistake.

Practice questions

1 Why do cool climates usually give wines with higher acidity?

Show the answer

Answer: Acidity falls more slowly as grapes ripen

Why: In cool climates grapes ripen slowly and keep more of their natural acidity.

2 What does malolactic conversion do to a wine?

Show the answer

Answer: Softens acidity and can add buttery notes

Why: Bacteria turn sharp malic acid into softer lactic acid; buttery aromas may appear.

3 Which method makes Champagne sparkling?

Show the answer

Answer: Traditional method: second fermentation in the bottle

Why: The second fermentation happens in the bottle, followed by ageing on the lees.

4 Sancerre is made mainly from which grape?

Show the answer

Answer: Sauvignon Blanc

Why: Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé in the Loire are classic Sauvignon Blanc.

5 What is a fortified wine?

Show the answer

Answer: A wine with grape spirit added

Why: Spirit is added during or after fermentation, as in Port and Sherry.

6 Barolo is made from which grape?

Show the answer

Answer: Nebbiolo

Why: Barolo and Barbaresco are 100% Nebbiolo: pale colour but high tannin and acidity.

7 Which region is famous for Malbec?

Show the answer

Answer: Mendoza

Why: Mendoza, in Argentina, is the world reference for Malbec.

8 What does 'Reserva' on a Rioja label mainly indicate?

Show the answer

Answer: A minimum period of ageing before release

Why: Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva are categories based on minimum ageing.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are in the WSET Level 2 exam?

The Level 2 exam has 50 multiple-choice questions to answer in one hour.

What is the pass mark for WSET Level 2?

You need 55% to pass. Higher results give a Pass with Merit or Distinction.

How long should I study for WSET Level 2?

Beyond the course, a few weeks of regular short sessions are usually enough; daily quizzes help you retain grapes and regions.
