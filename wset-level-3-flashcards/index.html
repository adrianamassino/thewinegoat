WSET Level 3 flashcards: grapes, regions, numbers and key terms

Flashcards are the quickest way to fix the hundreds of names, numbers and terms of the WSET Level 3 syllabus in your memory: grape varieties and their synonyms, appellations and their rules, ageing requirements, winemaking terms.

The Wine Goat has more than 2,500 flashcards in the free app – questions, terms, figures and grapes – with spaced repetition that shows you each card just before you would forget it. Flip a few below.

  • Little and often: 10 minutes of flashcards a day beats a long session once a week.
  • Say the answer out loud before flipping the card.
  • Keep the cards you miss in a separate pile and review them first.
  • Mix topics: it makes recall harder now and easier on exam day.
  • Use flashcards for facts and practice questions for reasoning.

Frequently asked questions

Are there free WSET Level 3 flashcards?

Yes, The Wine Goat flashcards on grapes, regions, terms and figures are free in the app, with spaced repetition.

How many flashcards do I need for WSET 3?

Enough to cover grapes, regions, labelling terms and key figures: The Wine Goat has more than 2,500, organised by topic.

Are flashcards enough to pass WSET Level 3?

No: they fix the facts, but the exam also tests reasoning and written answers, so combine them with practice questions and short answer questions.
