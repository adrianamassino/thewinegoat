WSET Level 3 mock exam: free practice test

A mock exam is the closest thing to exam day: the same mix of topics, the same time pressure and the same traps. Doing several before the WSET Level 3 exam shows you where you lose marks and trains your timing for the 50 multiple-choice questions and the 4 written answers.

Try the mini mock below: 10 questions from different chapters, marked instantly. In the free app you can take full timed mock exams with multiple-choice questions, short written answers and a blind tasting note, as many times as you like.

  • Do your first mock exam early, not in the last week: it tells you what to study.
  • Recreate exam conditions: timer on, phone away, no notes.
  • After each mock, spend more time on the review than on the test itself.
  • Track your score by section; aim for a margin above 55% everywhere.
  • In the last two weeks, alternate mock exams with revision of your weak points.

Sample questions with answers

1 Which vine species produces nearly all wine grapes?

Show the answer

Answer: Vitis vinifera

Why: V. vinifera is the main Eurasian species, used to make wine for several thousand years.

2 In which year after planting does the first crop usually come?

Show the answer

Answer: The third year

Why: Young pre-grafted vines are often protected by plastic sleeves; irrigation is sometimes allowed for establishment.

3 Why does white juice usually spend little time on the skins?

Show the answer

Answer: To reduce the risk of oxidation

Why: Whole-bunch pressing limits contact further, gently, potentially giving purer, more delicate wines.

4 In red winemaking, when does pressing normally happen?

Show the answer

Answer: After fermentation

Why: Skins stay in the vat to extract colour and tannin. In reds MLF is standard practice.

5 Between which towns are the Northern Rhône vineyards?

Show the answer

Answer: Between Vienne in the north and Valence in the south

Why: They are a small share of Rhône production but home to many premium wines.

6 How are inexpensive Spanish reds often made to enhance fruit?

Show the answer

Answer: Unoaked, with semi-carbonic maceration

Why: The best almost always see oak, partly new; traditionally American, now French too.

7 What climate do most German wine regions have?

Show the answer

Answer: Cool continental

Why: Baden in the south is noticeably warmer. The long cool ripening gives sugar while retaining acidity.

8 What may appear on the label of a US wine outside any AVA?

Show the answer

Answer: A state (e.g. California) or county (e.g. Napa County) name

Why: Federal laws set minimum percentages for variety, vintage and AVA; many states have their own laws. Non-AVA rules are often more lenient.

9 In which towns must Sherry be matured?

Show the answer

Answer: Jerez, Sanlúcar or El Puerto

Why: Current rule: Jerez, Sanlúcar or El Puerto. A reform awaiting EU approval would allow ageing anywhere in the DO, except Fino in Sanlúcar (that is Manzanilla).

10 What are the four key geographical features of Chile's vineyards, west to east?

Show the answer

Answer: Pacific Ocean, Coastal Range, Central Valley, Andes

Why: Vineyards span over 900 km north-south but rarely 100 km east-west.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free WSET Level 3 mock exam?

Yes: the mini mock on this page is free, and the app includes mock exams with multiple-choice questions, written answers and tasting.

How long is the WSET Level 3 theory exam?

The theory exam combines 50 multiple-choice questions and 4 short written answer questions; check your course provider for the exact timing of your session.

How many mock exams should I do before WSET 3?

At least three or four full mock exams, spaced out, with a thorough review after each one.
