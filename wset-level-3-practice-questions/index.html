WSET Level 3 practice questions with answers

The theory part of the WSET Level 3 Award in Wines starts with 50 multiple-choice questions covering the whole syllabus: vineyard and winery, the wines of every major region, sparkling and fortified wines. The fastest way to get them right is to practise exam-style questions until the answers come without hesitation.

Below are sample WSET Level 3 practice questions from The Wine Goat bank, with the correct answer and the reason behind it. The free app has more than 2,000 of them, organised by region and topic, and brings back every question you get wrong until you master it.

  • Read the question to the end: many distractors are true statements that answer a different question.
  • Learn the cause-and-effect chains (climate → grape → style), not isolated facts.
  • Practise in short daily sessions: 20 questions a day beat 200 the night before.
  • Revise your mistakes first; they are the fastest marks you can win back.
  • Time yourself: 50 questions must leave time for the written answers.

Sample questions with answers

1 Which traditional French term is more often seen on labels instead of AOP?

Show the answer

Answer: Appellation d'origine contrôlée (AC/AOC)

Why: One plot may be covered by a generic and a more prestigious appellation; the producer chooses yearly.

2 How late can spring frosts occur in Chablis?

Show the answer

Answer: As late as May

Why: Another hazard is localised summer hail, which can destroy much of a crop.

3 Which yeast carries the alcoholic fermentation of wine through to the end?

Show the answer

Answer: Saccharomyces cerevisiae

Why: Saccharomyces cerevisiae tolerates alcohol and finishes the fermentation.

4 What five things does the vine need?

Show the answer

Answer: Heat, light, water, nutrients, CO2

Why: CO2 is always plentiful; heat, light, water and nutrients vary and affect yield and quality.

5 Which traditional Italian term corresponds to PGI?

Show the answer

Answer: IGT (Tipica)

Why: The PDO term is DOP, but DOC and DOCG are more widely seen.

6 By how much does the second fermentation raise a sparkling wine's alcohol?

Show the answer

Answer: About 1.2-1.3%

Why: So the aim is a dry 10-11% base wine from low-sugar, high-acid grapes with ripe, non-green flavours.

7 What is Australia's principal grape of either colour?

Show the answer

Answer: Shiraz

Why: Hunter and Barossa give full fruity Shiraz; Geelong and Heathcote leaner, peppery. With Cabernet it plays Merlot's Bordeaux role.

8 What are the Douro's three Port sub-regions?

Show the answer

Answer: Baixo Corgo, Cima Corgo, Douro Superior

Why: Baixo Corgo is coolest and wettest with the lightest wines; Cima Corgo has most top vineyards; Douro Superior is renowned. Hotter and drier eastward.

Frequently asked questions

How many multiple-choice questions are in the WSET Level 3 exam?

The theory exam has 50 multiple-choice questions and 4 short written answer questions. The tasting part is a blind tasting of two wines.

Where can I find WSET Level 3 practice questions with answers?

The Wine Goat has more than 2,000 practice questions with explanations, organised by region and topic, in English, Italian, French and Spanish. The Bordeaux module is free in full.

What is the pass mark for WSET Level 3?

You need 55% in the tasting and in each of the two theory sections. Results are Pass, Merit or Distinction.
